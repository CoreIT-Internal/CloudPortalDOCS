# Domains and publishing

An app on CoreIT Hosting gets an address from the platform. If you want the app to be reached on your own domain, add the domain on the app's page and CoreIT connects it.

## The platform address on CoreIT Hosting

Apps on CoreIT Hosting get an address on `coreitsite.app`:

- The production environment gets `<appname>.coreitsite.app`, for example `myapp.coreitsite.app`.
- Other environments get `<appname>-<environment>.coreitsite.app`, for example `myapp-staging.coreitsite.app`.

The address is formed from the app's name. If the name is already taken, the address gets a number, for example `myapp-2.coreitsite.app`. The address is always reached over `https://`.

Until the address responds, the app's page says "The address is being prepared". After that the address is shown as a link.

In other hosting environments, CoreIT puts the app into service for you. The app's page shows the address once the app is in service.

## Add a custom domain

Domains are in the **Domains** panel under the **Your app** heading on the app's page.

1. Open the app under **My apps**.
2. Enter the domain name in the **Hostname** field, for example `app.yourcompany.com`.
3. Select **Add domain**.

You need the **Change application settings** permission.

Adding the domain records what you want. CoreIT then connects the domain to the app. Until then the domain shows **Waiting for configuration**, with DNS **not requested** and certificate **not requested**.

When the domain is **Ready**, the app's page shows the custom domain as its address, instead of the platform's address.

::note
A hostname that CloudPortal itself already uses, for example your organization's own portal address, cannot be connected to an app. The domain is then not added to the list. Choose another hostname, or [contact support](/en/support/contact-support) if the connection should be moved.
::

## Status for domain, DNS and certificate

Each domain shows its status in three parts:

- **Domain** — Waiting for configuration, Waiting for DNS, Waiting for certificate, Ready or Failed.
- **DNS** — not requested, pending, created, verified or failed.
- **Certificate** — not requested, pending, issued or failed.

Select **Refresh status** on the domain's row to fetch the latest state.

If the domain's DNS is with another provider, CoreIT may ask you to add a DNS record there. The app's status may then show **Waiting for the domain to point here**.

::warning
How quickly a DNS change takes effect depends on DNS propagation and caching outside the portal. Expect it to take a while before a new domain works.
::

## Health checks

With a health check, the portal monitors that an address responds. You find **Health checks** under **Advanced** on the app's page.

1. Enter the address in the **URL** field, for example `https://app.yourcompany.com/`.
2. Select **Add health check**.

The portal then checks the address at regular intervals for a while. While it waits for a response, it says "Watching until live …". Each row shows when the address was last checked. Select **Check now** to check right away.

You need the **Change application settings** permission.

A recent check decides whether the app's status shows **Running normally** or **The app is not responding**. See [Your app](/en/app-hosting/deploy-app).
