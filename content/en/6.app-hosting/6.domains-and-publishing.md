An app on CoreIT Hosting gets an address from the platform. If you want the app to be reached on your own name, for example `www.yourcompany.com`, add the name on the app's page and add two DNS records. The platform does the rest: it checks the records, connects the name and sorts out the certificate.

## The platform address on CoreIT Hosting

Apps on CoreIT Hosting get an address on `coreitsite.app`:

- The production environment gets `<appname>.coreitsite.app`, for example `myapp.coreitsite.app`.
- Other environments get `<appname>-<environment>.coreitsite.app`, for example `myapp-staging.coreitsite.app`.

The address is formed from the app's name. If the name is already taken, the address gets a number, for example `myapp-2.coreitsite.app`. The address is always reached over `https://`. If you rename the app later, the address does not change.

Before the app has been published for the first time, the app's page shows which address it gets, see [Your app](/en/app-hosting/deploy-app#status-and-address). Until the address responds, it says "The address is being prepared". After that the address is shown as a link.

In other hosting environments, CoreIT puts the app into service for you. The app's page shows the address once the app is in service.

## Add a custom domain

The **Custom domains** panel is under the **Your app** heading on the app's page. The domains belong to the environment you have chosen under **Environment**.

1. Open the app under **My apps** on the **Web apps** page.
2. Choose the environment under **Environment**, if the app has several.
3. Enter the name in the field, for example `www.yourcompany.com`, and select **Add domain**.
4. Add the records the domain's card shows, where the domain's DNS is managed. See [The DNS records](#the-dns-records).
5. Wait. The card updates by itself while something is under way. Select **Check now** to check right away.

You need the **Change application settings** permission. An environment can have up to 10 custom domains.

The app always answers on its platform address too. Once a custom domain is **Active**, the status card shows it as the app's address.

### Names that cannot be added

- **The bare domain**, for example `yourcompany.com`: "Add a name under the domain, for example www. The bare domain is not supported yet."
- **Wildcards**, for example `*.yourcompany.com`. Add one name at a time.
- **IP addresses**. Enter a domain name.
- **Names in the platform's own zones**, for example under `coreitsite.app`. The app gets those automatically.
- **A name that is already in use** elsewhere on the platform, for example by another app, a redirect, MTA-STS or CloudPortal itself, such as your organization's own portal address. It then says "The name is already used elsewhere on the platform." Choose another name, or [contact support](/en/support/contact-support) if the connection should be moved.

## The DNS records

Under "Add these records where the domain's DNS is managed:", the domain's card normally shows two records. The name and the value each have their own **Copy** button.

| Type | Name | What the record does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TXT | `_cloudportal-verify.` followed by your name | Shows the domain is yours. The value starts with `cp-verify=`. |
| CNAME | your name, for example `www.yourcompany.com` | Sends the traffic to the app. |

Sometimes the card shows a third record, a TXT that Cloudflare needs to confirm the name. Add that one too.

### Let the portal create the records

If the domain is in a DNS zone your organization has here in the portal, the card says so and the **Create the records for me** button is shown. The portal then creates the records in the zone right away, and the card shows how it went for each record, for example **created** or **already there**.

- If the name already has another record, it is left untouched. The row then says "not created: the name already has another record, which was left untouched". Remove or change that record yourself, and then add the records from the card.
- If you do not have the right to change DNS records in the zone, the card says so. Add the records yourself, or ask someone who has the right. See [DNS records](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-records).

::warning
How quickly a new DNS record shows up depends on DNS propagation and caching outside the portal. Expect it to take a while before the platform finds it.
::

## The proof of ownership

From when you add the name, you have seven days to add the TXT record. The card shows when the time runs out: "The name is released … if the proof is not found." If the record is not found in time, the domain becomes **Expired** and the name is released. Select **Add again** once the record is in place.

While the platform waits for the proof, the other steps say "after the proof".

## Steps and status

Each domain shows five steps in the order they happen. Each step is done, waiting, failed or after the proof.

1. **Ownership** — the TXT record has been found.
2. **DNS** — the CNAME record points at the app.
3. **Connection** — the platform has connected the name with Cloudflare.
4. **Certificate** — the certificate for the name has been issued.
5. **Reachable** — the name answers from the app.

The domain's status is shown next to the name:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting proof of ownership** | The TXT record has not been found yet. |
| **Being prepared** | Ownership is proven, and the platform is preparing the name. |
| **Awaiting DNS** | The CNAME record is missing or points elsewhere. |
| **Certificate being issued** | Cloudflare confirms the name and issues the certificate. |
| **Active** | The app answers on the name. |
| **Disrupted, being repaired** | The name was active, but something no longer holds. The platform is repairing it. |
| **Failed** | Something the platform cannot repair by itself. |
| **Expired** | The proof did not come in time, and the name has been released. |
| **Being removed** / **Removed** | The name is being removed, or has been removed. |

When the reason is something you can act on, it is shown below the steps, for example "The CNAME record for the name does not point at the address above yet. Change it and the rest continues by itself." If the reason is the platform's, for example that it has reached its ceiling for custom domains, you do not need to change anything in your DNS.

## Remove a domain

1. Select **Remove** on the domain's card.
2. Confirm. "The app stops answering on the name. You can add it again later."

The domain shows **Being removed** until the name has been removed everywhere on the platform. Until then, the name cannot be added again.

## Health checks

With a health check, the portal monitors that an address responds. You find **Health checks** under **Advanced** on the app's page.

1. Enter the address in the **URL** field, for example `https://app.yourcompany.com/`.
2. Select **Add health check**.

The portal then checks the address at regular intervals for a while. While it waits for a response, it says "Watching until live …". Each row shows when the address was last checked. Select **Check now** to check right away.

You need the **Change application settings** permission.

A recent check decides whether the app's status shows **Running normally** or **The app is not responding**. See [Your app](/en/app-hosting/deploy-app).
