# Manage zones

With **Manage zones** you decide which of the organization's zones are shown in the zone list. There you activate new zones, hide zones you don't want to see and clean up zones the organization no longer reaches.

Most zones need no action from you. Zones the organization owns, or that are shared with yours, appear in the zone list on their own. See [How zones get into the list](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-zones#how-zones-get-into-the-list).

::note
You need the **Manage zone ownership** and **Find the organization's zones (first-time setup and Manage zones)** permissions. Without them the button is not shown.
::

## Open Manage zones

1. Go to **Domains** → **DNS** → **Zones**.
2. Click **Manage zones**. A side panel opens with the zones linked to the organization.
3. Click **Rescan** if you want to look for zones again.

The zones are split across the tabs **All**, **Active**, **Available**, **Hidden** and **No access**. The last tab is only shown when there are such zones.

## Status

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The zone is shown in the zone list. |
| **Available** | The organization reaches the zone, but it is not activated. |
| **Hidden** | The organization has hidden the zone. It is not shown in the zone list. |
| **No access** | The organization no longer reaches the zone. It may have been moved or deleted, be owned by another account, or a share has ended. |

## Actions

Select one or more zones. The buttons shown depend on what you have selected:

- **Activate** — available zones are shown in the zone list.
- **Show** — hidden zones are shown in the zone list again.
- **Hide** — the zones are no longer shown in the zone list and cannot be used in the portal. You are asked to confirm. The zones remain on the name servers, and you can show them again.
- **Delete** — permanently deletes the zones, with all DNS records. Only applies to active zones the organization owns. You must tick "I understand that this action cannot be undone." before you can confirm.
- **Remove from list** — for zones with the status **No access**. The zone disappears from the list. Nothing changes on the DNS server.

::warning
**Delete** and **Remove from list** are different things. **Delete** removes the zone and its records from the name servers and cannot be undone. **Remove from list** only tidies the organization's list.
::

## If a zone shows No access

The organization no longer reaches the zone, and it is not shown in the zone list. If the zone should be yours, contact CoreIT.

A zone that is temporarily unreachable is not forgotten. It is not shown in the meantime, but comes back when the organization reaches it again.

::note
The former **Autodiscover** page no longer exists. What it did now happens automatically when the zone list is opened, and the rest is done in Manage zones.
::
