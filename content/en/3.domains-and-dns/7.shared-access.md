# Shared access

A DNS zone can be shared with another organization. The organization that owns the zone keeps responsibility for it. The recipient can read the zone, or read and change its records, depending on the share.

Shared access applies to zones in the **DNS** module. It is not the same as a [delegation](/en/organization/delegations). A delegation lends permissions to a person at your provider. Shared access gives another organization access to a single zone.

::note
Shares are added and removed by CoreIT. Contact CoreIT if you want to share a zone with another organization or end a share.
::

## Levels

| Level | The recipient can |
|---|---|
| **Read** | Read the zone and its records. |
| **Manage records** | Read and change records. |

A share lasts either until further notice or until a given date.

The recipient's own permissions also apply. A person can never do more than both the share and their role in the organization allow. To change records in a zone shared with the **Manage records** level, for example, the person also needs the **Edit DNS records** permission.

## When a zone is shared with you

The zone appears in the ordinary zone list, marked with the level and any end date, for example **Shared access · Read · until 2026-12-31**. It appears on its own once the share has been added.

On the zone page a box explains that the zone is owned by another organization, what you can do and how long access lasts.

**You can:**

- see the zone and its records
- export the zone, if you have the **Export DNS zones** permission
- with the **Manage records** level: create, change and delete records, change several records at once and import a zone file, if you have the permissions for it
- see the zone's changes under **Changes**

**You cannot:**

- delete the zone
- manage redirects, parking, email security or other add-ons for the zone; they belong to the owner
- change the owner's system records, for example records managed by the owner's redirects or email security. They are shown, but locked: "Managed by the zone's owner. Cannot be changed through shared access."

### When the share changes or ends

The portal checks access on every action. If the share has changed or ended, the next action is refused, even if the page is already open.

When the share has ended, the zone disappears from the zone list. In [Manage zones](/en/domains-and-dns/manage-zones) it shows as **No access**. The zone's history is then no longer shown to you either, not even your own changes.

## When you own a zone that is shared

On the zone page, below the records, the **Shared access** panel shows which organizations the zone is shared with:

- the organization's name
- the level, **Read** or **Manage records**
- how long the share lasts: a date, **until further notice**, or **expired**

The panel is only shown when the zone is actually shared, and only to those with the **Manage zone ownership** permission.

Changes the recipients make appear in [Recent changes](/en/domains-and-dns/recent-changes) and in the record's change history. They are marked with the recipient's organization, for example **Example Ltd (via shared access)**, without the person's name. A recipient never sees which other organizations the zone is shared with.
