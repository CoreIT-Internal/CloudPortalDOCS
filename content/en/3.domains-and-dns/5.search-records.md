# Search records

**Search records** looks for a record in all of the organization's zones at once. The result shows where the record exists and, just as important, which zones lack it. This is useful when you want to check that every domain has the same SPF record, or find every record that points to an old IP address.

You find the page under **Domains** → **DNS** → **Search records**. The same page is available in **Cloudflare DNS**. You need the **View DNS zones and records** permission.

## Search

1. Fill in one or more fields:
   - **Name** — the record's name.
   - **Content (value or target)** — for example an IP address, a CNAME target or text in a TXT record.
   - **Type** — one or more of A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, SRV, CAA and NS.
2. Choose how name and content should match: **Contains**, **Exact** or **Starts with**.
3. Click **Search** or press Enter.

Every condition you filled in must match. The search is kept in the address bar, so you can save or share the link.

::tip
The search compares what records mean, not just the characters. Upper and lower case don't matter, an IPv6 address is found however it is written, and trailing dots and quotes in TXT records don't affect the match. Names with international characters are found in both the ordinary form and the ASCII form.
::

## The result

- **Hits per zone.** Hits are grouped per zone, with a count. Each hit shows type, name and value, with the part that matched highlighted. You can copy the value, and click the hit to open the record on the zone page.
- **Zones.** A panel shows how many zones have been searched and the status of each zone:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Hit** | The zone has at least one matching record. |
| **No hit** | The zone was searched and has no matching record. |
| **Incomplete** | The zone could not be read to the end, for example because it is larger than one search reads or because the search was cancelled. |
| **Could not search** | The zone could not be searched right now. |
| **Searching** / **Not searched yet** | The search is in progress. |

Click **Cancel** to stop a running search. **Retry failed and incomplete zones** searches again in the zones that did not finish.

::note
A result without hits is only certain when every zone has been searched. It then says "No hits in any zone". If it instead says "No hits so far – not every zone was searched", some zones could not be searched.
::

## Example: which domains lack SPF?

1. Choose the type **TXT**.
2. Type `v=spf1` under **Content (value or target)** and choose **Starts with**.
3. Click **Search**. Zones with the status **No hit** lack an SPF record.

## Which zones are searched

The search goes through the zones in the organization's zone list, including zones shared with yours. Zones are searched one at a time.

- **DNS:** if a DNS server is busy, the search waits and tries again. If that does not succeed, the zone shows "The DNS server is busy".
- **Cloudflare DNS:** every zone needs calls to Cloudflare, and searches may use a quarter of the connection's calls. A large search therefore asks you to confirm first (**Search anyway**). If the calls run out, the remaining zones are marked incomplete.
