# DNS records

DNS records control how your domain works: where web traffic and email go, how services are verified and more. You manage records inside a zone. First open the zone from [DNS zones](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-zones).

## Find the right records

On the zone page the records are shown under **Records**, in a table with the columns **Name**, **Type**, **Value** and **TTL**.

- Use the **Search name, value or type** field.
- Click a record type in the type filter to show only that type. Each type shows how many records it has. On mobile the type filter is under **Filter**.
- Click the copy button in the value cell to copy the value.

To find a record in every zone at once, use [Search records](/en/domains-and-dns/search-records).

## Create a record

1. Click **New record**. The **New record** panel opens.
2. Choose a type: **A**, **AAAA**, **CNAME**, **MX**, **TXT**, **SRV**, **CAA** or **TLSA**.
3. Fill in the name. `@` means the zone root, and a prefix such as `www` gives a subdomain. Under the field you see the full name the record is saved as.
4. Fill in the value. The fields adapt to the type:
   - **A** — an IPv4 address, for example `192.0.2.1`
   - **AAAA** — an IPv6 address, for example `2001:db8::1`
   - **CNAME** — a target, for example `target.example.com`
   - **MX** — a mail server and a priority
   - **TXT** — a text value, for example an SPF line
   - **SRV**, **CAA** and **TLSA** have their own fields for their parts
5. Enter the TTL, that is how long the answer may be cached.
6. Optionally write a **Comment (optional)**. It is an internal note that is not visible in DNS, at most 1,500 characters.
7. Click **Create**, or **Save and create another** to continue with the next record.

::note
NS and PTR records cannot be created in the portal.
::

## Change and delete records

- **Change:** click **Edit** on the record, make the change and click **Save**. A record cannot change type in place. If you change the type, the old record is removed and the new one created, and if that fails the old one is put back.
- **Delete:** open the record's menu (**…**) and choose **Delete**. Confirm in the **Delete this record?** dialog. The record is removed from the name servers immediately, and this cannot be undone.
- **Show in full:** in the same menu, **Show in full** shows long values in their entirety.

### Several records at once

Click **Select records**, select the records and choose **Delete** or **Change TTL**. The change appears as a single row in [Recent changes](/en/domains-and-dns/recent-changes), which can be expanded.

## If a change did not go through

The portal tells you what happened when a change could not be completed:

- **"The change was refused. Nothing changed."**
- **"The change could not be saved. The original record has been restored."**
- **"The record changed after the form was opened."** Someone else changed the record in the meantime. Open it again and redo your change.
- **"It is not known whether the change was saved."** The DNS server did not answer in a way that shows what happened. Check the record in the list, for example with **Check again**, before trying again. Otherwise another attempt could create a duplicate.
- **"The record could neither be saved nor restored."** The portal shows the original and the requested values so you can create the record again.

## Rules to know

Some combinations are not allowed in DNS, and the portal then shows an error:

- A **CNAME** cannot be at the zone root (`@`). Use an A or AAAA record instead, or a name such as `www`.
- A **CNAME** must be alone on its name. It cannot exist together with other records on the same name.

::note
The SOA record and the zone's name servers are shown under **Zone details** and are managed automatically.
::

## Locked records

Some records are managed by another feature and are locked in the record table. The lock icon explains why.

- **Redirect** — the record is managed by the redirect service. See [Redirects](/en/domains-and-dns/redirects).
- **MTA-STS** — the record is managed by the email security module.

The badge on the record links to the feature that manages it. Change the record there.

In a zone shared with yours, the owner's system records are also locked: "Managed by the zone's owner. Cannot be changed through shared access." See [Shared access](/en/domains-and-dns/shared-access).

## Change history for a record

When a record has been changed, an information icon is shown on its row. Click it to see when the record was last changed (**Last changed**) and by whom (**By**). The full history is in [Recent changes](/en/domains-and-dns/recent-changes).

## International characters

Record names and targets, for example for CNAME, MX and SRV, can be written with characters such as å, ä and ö. Under the field you see how the name is written in DNS. See the section on international characters under [DNS zones](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-zones).

## Downstream effects

::note
The portal publishes your DNS records, but how quickly a change takes effect is decided by caching (TTL) at other DNS servers and resolvers along the way. Expect changes to take time to take effect everywhere.
::
