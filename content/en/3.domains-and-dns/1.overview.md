# Domains & DNS

Under **Domains** in the portal there are two DNS modules:

- **DNS** — zones hosted on CoreIT's name servers.
- **Cloudflare DNS** — zones in the organization's own Cloudflare account.

## What you can do in DNS

The **DNS** module has four pages in the menu: **Zones**, **Search records**, **Recent changes** and **Redirects**.

- **[DNS zones](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-zones)** — see the organization's zones, create a zone, export and import zone files and delete a zone.
- **[Manage zones](/en/domains-and-dns/manage-zones)** — decide which zones are shown in the list.
- **[DNS records](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-records)** — create, change and delete records such as A, AAAA, CNAME, MX and TXT.
- **[Search records](/en/domains-and-dns/search-records)** — find a record in every zone at once, and see which zones lack it.
- **[Recent changes](/en/domains-and-dns/recent-changes)** — see what changed, by whom and with what outcome.
- **[Shared access](/en/domains-and-dns/shared-access)** — zones another organization owns and shares with yours, or that you share with others.
- **[Redirects](/en/domains-and-dns/redirects)** — forward a domain or create custom redirects, and test where an address leads.
- **[RewriteMaps](/en/domains-and-dns/rewritemaps)** — collect many redirects in a map and publish them together.
- **[Import redirects](/en/domains-and-dns/import-redirects)** — import and export redirects, in one zone or for several zones.

**Search records** and **Recent changes** are also available in **Cloudflare DNS**. How Cloudflare zones differ is described under [Cloudflare zones](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-zones#cloudflare-zones).

## Domain names with international characters

You can type domain names with characters such as å, ä and ö, and the portal shows them that way. In DNS they are stored in an ASCII form that starts with `xn--`. See the section on international characters under [DNS zones](/en/domains-and-dns/dns-zones).

## Permissions

What you can do depends on your role in the organization. The permissions for the DNS module:

| Permission | Gives |
|---|---|
| **View DNS zones and records** | The zone list, the zone page, Search records and Recent changes |
| **Edit DNS records** | Create, change and delete records |
| **Export DNS zones** | Export zone files |
| **Import DNS records** | Import a zone file (also requires Edit DNS records) |
| **Create DNS zones** | Create a new zone |
| **Edit DNS zones** | Delete a zone |
| **Manage zone ownership** | Manage zones, and see who a zone is shared with |
| **Find the organization's zones (first-time setup and Manage zones)** | Also required for Manage zones |

The permissions for redirects are described under [Redirects](/en/domains-and-dns/redirects). See also [Roles and permissions](/en/organization/roles-and-permissions).
