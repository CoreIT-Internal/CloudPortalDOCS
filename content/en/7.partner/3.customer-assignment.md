# Customer assignment

You connect customers to the right partner by creating them in the right place. As a distributor you create providers; as a provider you create customer organizations. Both are done in the same wizard:

**Identity → Details → Link and owner → Summary**

Once the customer has been created, a confirmation shows what happened.

## Start the wizard

- **New provider (as a distributor):** go to **Providers** and click **Create provider**, or choose **Create provider** under **Tenant management** on the partner home page.
- **New organization (as a provider):** go to **Organizations** and click **Create organization**, or choose **Create organization** under **Tenant management**.

You need the right to create in the chosen place: **Create providers** at a distributor, **Create organizations** at a provider. The tenant's administrators hold them.

## Step 1: Identity

"Which company is it?"

1. Check the placement first:
   - for a provider, **Distributors** shows the distributor you are in
   - for an organization, choose which **Provider** it belongs to (preselected when you start from a provider)
2. Enter the **Organisation or personal identity number**. Ten digits for a company, for example 556677-8899. A sole trader enters a twelve-digit personal identity number.
3. The portal checks the number against Bolagsverket and shows the answer right away.

| Answer | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Verified at Bolagsverket** | The company exists and is active. Name, address and status are shown. You can continue. |
| **Already in CloudPortal** | The company already exists here. Click **Open** to go to it instead of creating a duplicate. |
| **The company exists, but is not active** | For example deregistered or in liquidation. You must tick "I have checked that this is the right company, despite its status." to continue. |
| **Personal identity number (sole trader)** | Personal identity numbers are not checked against the company register. |
| **The number is not registered at Bolagsverket** | The number could not be confirmed. Depending on your rights, you can create anyway with a **Reason**, which is kept in the log, or you are told "The number could not be confirmed. Ask your distributor for help." |
| **The details could not be verified just now** | The register did not answer. Try again, or tick "I understand the details are not checked against Bolagsverket." to continue. |

::note
Companies without a Swedish organisation number cannot be created yet.
::

If the company is the same legal party as a level further up the hierarchy, it cannot be placed there. If it is the same company as the chosen distributor or provider, you confirm that in step 3.

Click **Next**.

## Step 2: Details

Registered details come from the source and cannot be changed here.

- **Registered name** and **Registered address** are shown read-only, with source and date.
- **Display name** — the name shown in the portal. It is required. Click **Use the registered name** to use the register's name.
- **Billing email** (organizations only) — optional. Where invoices and receipts are sent.

## Step 3: Link and owner

1. Check the distributor or provider the customer is linked to.
2. If the customer is the same legal company as the chosen level, **Same legal company** is shown. Tick "Confirm that this is the same company in two portal roles" — for example when a distributor is also a provider — or go back to step 1 and choose another placement.
3. Enter the **Owner email**. The owner gets an invitation, or a confirmation if the account already exists.

## Step 4: Summary

Check the details before creating. The summary shows **Identity**, **Company**, **Link**, **Owner** and **Special choices**, for example a reason or a same-company confirmation.

Click **Create**.

## Done

The confirmation "{name} was created" shows what actually happened with the email to the owner:

- "The owner has been sent an invitation by email." or "The owner has been emailed about the new account."
- "The invitation is saved, but the email could not be sent." Use the link **Resend the invitation from the members page**.
- "The owner has been added, but the confirmation email could not be sent."

Click **Open** to go to the new customer. After a new provider you can go straight to **Create the first organisation**.

::tip
If you submit the same form twice, for example by clicking again, nothing is created twice. You are told "This was already created earlier from the same form."
::

## After creation

- **The identity cannot be changed.** The organisation number and registered details are shown in the **Identity** panel and cannot be changed after creation. Check the number before you create.
- **Creating a customer does not give your staff access.** Access to the customer comes from an MSP assignment. An assignment with the scope **A provider's customers** or **The distributor's whole customer tree** also covers new customers. See [Roles and permissions](/en/partner/roles-and-permissions).

## Archive and deactivate

- A provider can be **archived** to take it out of use and **restored** when needed. See [Tenants and organizations](/en/partner/tenants-and-organizations).
- Organizations can be active or inactive. An inactive organization remains but is not in use.
