# Partner

The partner section is for MSP partners who manage several customer organizations in CloudPortal. You open it with **Partner Admin** under **Settings** in the menu bar.

## The home page

The home page shows the tenant you are in, with counters for providers or organizations, members and active modules.

- **Common tasks** — "What you do most often in {tenant}. Every page is in the menu." Here you find, for example, **Customers you can reach**, **Create organization** and **Invite member**. You only see the tasks your permissions allow.
- **Operations & news** — incidents and news for your customers. See [Support flows](/en/partner/support-flows).
- **System health** — "The platform’s ability to deliver — not individual customer configuration." **View details** opens more information.

## The tenant's menu

The tenant's pages are in the menu on the left. You only see the pages you have permission for.

- **Overview**
- **Access:** **Members**, **MSP roles**, **Role templates** (distributors only) and **Auth & SSO**
- **Services:** **Modules**, **Email** and **Support**
- **Appearance:** **Branding** and **Domain**
- **Audit:** **Logs**

## What you can do

- **Tenants & organizations** — administer the customers you're responsible for. The customer catalog shows all of the tenant's customers, including those you cannot open.
- **Customer assignment** — create providers and customer organizations.
- **Roles and permissions** — control what your team may do in the tenant and at the customers.
- **Customer access** — give a person an MSP role with a scope, so that they reach the customers. The tenant role opens no customer.
- **Sign-in and SCIM** — require SSO and two-step verification for your staff, and appoint recovery administrators.
- **Modules** — control which modules the organizations under you get.
- **Support flows** — keep your customers informed about disruptions and news.

## Configure a customer's organization

You set up SSO, SCIM, custom domain and branding for a customer in the customer's own organization. Select the customer in the organization switcher and follow the same steps as for an organization:

- [Single sign-on (SSO)](/en/organization/sso)
- [SCIM provisioning](/en/organization/scim)
- [Custom domain](/en/organization/custom-domain)
- [Branding](/en/organization/branding)

::note
This requires an MSP assignment whose role grants **Organization administration** in the customer. See [Customer access](/en/partner/roles-and-permissions).
::
