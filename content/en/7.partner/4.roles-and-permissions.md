# Roles and permissions

Access for the partner's team is built from three parts that are kept separate:

- **Tenant role** — what the person may do in your own tenant. It opens no customer.
- **MSP role** — a description of what a consultant may do at the customers. Defining a role grants no access.
- **Customer access** — an MSP role assigned to a person, with a scope: the customers the role applies in. The assignment is what opens customers.

In addition, a customer can lend permissions to your staff with a delegation.

## Tenant roles

Every member of the tenant has a tenant role. You view and change the roles under the tenant's **Members**.

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Administers the tenant, its members, MSP roles and assignments. |
| **User** | Sees the tenant and the customer catalog. |
| **Support** | Sees the tenant, the customer catalog and the tenant's log. |
| **Viewer** | Sees the tenant, but not the customer catalog. |

### Invite a member

1. Go to the tenant's **Members** and click **Invite member**.
2. Enter the **Email**.
3. Choose a role from the role cards. **Viewer** is preselected.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

::note
The tenant role opens no customer. Customer access is granted with an MSP assignment once the person has joined.
::

Unanswered invitations are listed under **Pending invitations**. There you can choose **Resend** or **Cancel**.

You change a member's role directly in the list. There you can also **Disable** or remove a member.

::warning
You cannot remove your own administrator role. That change must be made by another administrator.
::

## MSP roles

An MSP role describes what a consultant may do at the customers. Defining a role grants no access — an assignment with a scope under **Members** does (see Customer access below).

Both providers and distributors define their own MSP roles in their tenant. The roles are not predefined — you build them as needed. You need the right **Define MSP roles**, which the tenant's administrators hold.

### Create an MSP role

1. Open the tenant's **MSP roles**.
2. Click **Create MSP role**.
3. Enter a **Name** and a **Description**.
4. Choose what the role grants in the two parts below.
5. Click **Save**.

**In customers** applies in every customer organization an assignment of the role reaches.

- **Read the organization** is always included.
- Choose the organization permissions the role should grant, for example **Organization administration**.
- Under **Modules** you choose permissions per module. Each module shows how many are selected, for example "2 of 5". You can search modules and permissions.

**In the tenant {name}** covers administration of the tenant itself, not of the customers. You can only hand out what you hold yourself.

Permissions marked **Sensitive** change who has access, reach secrets or affect more than one organization. Assigning a role with such permissions takes a special right (see Sensitive permissions below).

The role list shows what each role grants **In customers** and **In the tenant**, and how many members use it. You can **Edit** or **Delete** a role.

### MSP role templates

A distributor can offer ready-made role templates to its providers.

- **As a distributor** you open the template library through the **Role templates for providers →** link on the MSP roles page, or through **MSP role templates** under **Tenant management** on the partner home page.
- **As a provider** you click **Create from template...** on the MSP roles page. When the template gets a new version you can **Upgrade** the role.

::tip
If you see "No templates available. Contact your distributor." the template library is empty. Ask your distributor to fill it.
::

## Customer access

Customer access is an MSP role assigned to a person, with a scope. The tenant role grants no access to customers.

In provider and distributor tenants the member table has the **Customer access** column. It shows each assignment as role and scope, for example "Customers of {provider}", or **No customer access**.

### Grant customer access

You need the right **Assign MSP roles**, which the tenant's administrators hold.

1. Go to the tenant's **Members**.
2. Click **Grant customer access** on the person's row. If the person already has customer access, the button is called **Change**.
3. Under **New assignment**, choose the **Role**.
4. Choose the **Scope**:
   - **A provider's customers** — "Every customer of the chosen provider, including future ones." A distributor also chooses the provider.
   - **The distributor's whole customer tree** — distributors only. Every customer of the providers the distributor may serve as an MSP, including future ones.
   - **Selected organizations** — "Only the organizations you choose. Least privilege." Search for and tick the customers.
5. Check what you are granting. The dialog shows what the role grants in every customer it reaches, and how many organizations the scope covers today.
6. Click **Assign**.

A person can have several assignments. They are all shown in the **Customer access for {name}** dialog:

- **Change scope** changes the scope. To change the role, remove the assignment and create a new one.
- **Remove** takes two clicks. The button first changes to "Click again to remove".

::note
The person reaches the customers through the context switcher. The organizations are listed under your tenant, and inside a customer the top bar shows which tenant the person is acting as. See [Select organization](/en/getting-started/select-organization).
::

### Sensitive permissions

A role with sensitive permissions can only be assigned by someone who holds the right **Grant sensitive permissions**. The tenant's administrators hold it. If you lack the right, the dialog says so before you assign.

### Rules

- **Nobody grants themselves customer access.** Your own row has no **Grant customer access** button.
- **Only active members** can be given new customer access.
- **Assignments held by SCIM** are marked **SCIM**. If you remove the manual source, the assignment remains for as long as the SCIM group grants it. See [SCIM provisioning](/en/organization/scim).

::warning
Choose the narrowest scope that is enough. **A provider's customers** and **The distributor's whole customer tree** also cover customers added later.
::

## Delegations

A customer can lend some of its module permissions, for a limited time, to a person at your company. How it works is described in [Delegations](/en/organization/delegations).

The same flow is available in the partner section under **Organizations → [customer]**, on the delegations tab.

::note
If the person already has an active delegation in the organization, the new delegation replaces the existing one.
::
