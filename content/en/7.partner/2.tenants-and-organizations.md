# Tenants and organizations

In the partner section you administer the structure of tenants and customer organizations you are responsible for. This page describes how the structure fits together and how you work with it in the portal.

## How the structure fits together

The partner structure has three levels:

- **Distributor** — the top partner level. A distributor can create and manage several providers.
- **Provider** — an MSP partner that delivers services to end customers. A provider creates and manages customer organizations.
- **Organization** — an end customer. Organizations always belong to a provider.

Which actions you see in the portal depend on the type of tenant you are logged in to. As a distributor you work mainly with providers; as a provider you work mainly with organizations.

::note
If you manage several tenants, you switch between them with the context switcher. The tenant you are in determines which customers and actions are shown.
::

## Find your partner overview

1. Open the partner section in the portal.
2. On the home page you see the tenant you are in, with quick statistics on the number of organizations (or providers), members and active modules.
3. Click a number to go directly to the corresponding list.
4. Under **Tenant management** you find quick links to members, email settings, modules, MSP roles (or MSP role templates as a distributor), branding and login domain.

::tip
Click **View details** to open the full view for the current tenant, or **View tenant tree** to see the whole structure.
::

## Manage providers (as a distributor)

As a distributor you see your providers in the provider list.

1. Go to **Providers** in the partner section.
2. Search by name and filter by status: **Active**, **Archived** or **All**.
3. Click a provider to open its detail view. It contains the **Identity** panel (see below).
4. Use **Archive** or **Restore** to take a provider out of use or reactivate it.

How to create new providers is described in [Customer assignment](/en/partner/customer-assignment).

### Which providers you administer

A provider being linked to your distributor is a business relationship. It grants nothing by itself. You only administer the providers your distributor is allowed to administer — for example their members, settings and organizations. For other providers you cannot make changes.

In the same way, you reach a provider's customers as an MSP only if the distributor may serve that provider's customers. Those are the providers you can choose when granting customer access. See [Roles and permissions](/en/partner/roles-and-permissions).

## Manage organizations (as a provider)

As a provider you see your customer organizations in the organization list.

1. Go to **Organizations** in the partner section.
2. Search by name or slug to find the right customer.
3. Each row shows the number of members and the status (active or inactive).
4. Click an organization to open its overview. From there you reach the tabs for members, **Auth & SSO**, email and delegations.

::note
The list shows the organizations that belong to the tenant you are in. If the list is empty, you may need to switch to the right provider first.
::

### The customer catalog

The organization list is also the tenant's customer catalog. If your tenant role sees the catalog, the list shows all of the tenant's customers, including ones you cannot open.

- A customer you can open links to its overview.
- A customer that is visible only through the catalog is marked **Catalog** and cannot be opened: "You can see that this customer exists. Opening it takes an MSP assignment or a membership in the organization."

The tenant roles **Administrator**, **User** and **Support** see the catalog. **Viewer** does not.

::tip
Seeing a customer in the catalog gives no access to it. Access is granted with customer access (an MSP assignment), see [Roles and permissions](/en/partner/roles-and-permissions).
::

## Identity

A provider's detail view and an organization's overview have the **Identity** panel. It is read-only ("Cannot be changed here.") and shows:

- **Organisation number** (or **Personal identity number** for a sole trader).
- **Check** — for example "Verified at Bolagsverket", with the date of the check.
- **Registered name** and **Registered address** from the register.

The identity is set when the customer is created and cannot be changed afterwards. See [Customer assignment](/en/partner/customer-assignment).

## Tenant settings

From a tenant's detail view you reach settings that affect how the customer experiences the portal:

- **Members** — which people have access and with which role, and their customer access. See [Roles and permissions](/en/partner/roles-and-permissions).
- **Email** — sender and email configuration.
- **Modules** — which features are enabled.
- **Auth & SSO** — SSO and sign-in rules.
- **MSP roles** (providers) — what the consultants may do at the customers.
- **Branding** — customized appearance.
- **Domain** — a dedicated domain for sign-in.
- **Logs** — review activity in the tenant.

::warning
Some settings, such as a dedicated login domain, require DNS to be repointed. The portal can publish and verify what lies within the portal, but the final effect depends on external DNS servers and providers. Expect that it may take time before changes take effect everywhere.
::
