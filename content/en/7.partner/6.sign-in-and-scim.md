# Sign-in and SCIM

Under the tenant's **Auth & SSO**, a provider or distributor controls how its own staff sign in. The settings apply to the tenant itself. You set up a customer's sign-in in the customer's own organization, see [Single sign-on (SSO)](/en/organization/sso).

The page is in the menu for those who can administer the tenant. Changes are saved with **Save changes**.

## Connect Microsoft Entra ID

You connect the tenant to Microsoft Entra ID the same way as an organization: verify the domain, complete the connection and sign in through it once. The steps are described in [Single sign-on (SSO)](/en/organization/sso).

## Require SSO

"Anyone with an address in the tenant's verified domains must sign in via SSO. Recovery administrators are the exception, while password sign-in is allowed for them."

Before SSO can be required, the tenant needs at least one verified SSO domain, and someone must have signed in through the connection at least once.

When the tenant requires SSO, the tenant is locked in the organization switcher for anyone who signed in another way. The customers you reach through the tenant are only shown after a sign-in via the tenant's SSO. See [Select organization](/en/getting-started/select-organization).

## Allow password for recovery administrators

"Recovery administrators sign in with password and MFA even when SSO is required, for example when Entra is down or has removed the last administrator. Without it they can sign in only via SSO."

If you turn it off while SSO is required, you must tick "I confirm there is another administrative way in": "Password sign-in for recovery administrators is turned off. If SSO stops working you could otherwise be locked out of the portal."

## Recovery administrator

A recovery administrator is the way back in if SSO or Entra stops working.

1. Go to the tenant's **Members**.
2. Choose **Make recovery admin** on an active member with the **Administrator** role who does not come from Entra.
3. Confirm "Make {email address} a recovery administrator?".

"A recovery administrator signs in with password and MFA even when the tenant requires SSO and Entra is not working, and SCIM cannot change or turn off the account. Use a dedicated account outside Entra provisioning." The account needs a password and two-step verification.

The member is marked **Recovery admin** in the list. **Remove as recovery admin** removes it, but "The last recovery administrator cannot be removed. Appoint a new one first."

## Require MFA

"Your staff, and a distributor's who administer you, must have two-step verification when they sign in with a password. Anyone without it sets it up at the next sign-in. A sign-in through SSO counts as met."

What it looks like for the person signing in is described in [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in).

## Organization assignment on first SSO login

The setting decides what happens when someone signs in via the tenant's SSO for the first time. "Applies only to someone who signs in via the tenant's SSO without any membership in the tenant or its customers. Someone with an active membership in the tenant, for example from SCIM, always lands in the tenant. Someone whose membership has been turned off, by an administrator or by Entra, is never placed automatically."

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Disabled (recommended with SCIM)** | Nobody is placed automatically. Someone without a membership is not let in, and no account is created. Access is given by invitation, SCIM or an MSP assignment. |
| **Domain matching** | The person is placed at the customer whose verified SSO domain matches their email domain, with the customer's default role. If that default role is Admin or Owner, or no domain matches, the person is not let in. |
| **Domain matching + fallback organization** | Like domain matching, but someone who matches no customer's domain is placed in the **Fallback organization** with its default role. The organization must be active, belong to the tenant and not have Admin or Owner as its default role. |

## SCIM

With SCIM provisioning, Entra ID creates, deactivates and syncs the tenant's users and groups. You turn it on and create a token the same way as in an organization, see [SCIM provisioning](/en/organization/scim).

### Group–role mappings in the tenant

In a tenant, a group can be mapped to two things. An organization role cannot be chosen here.

- **MSP role** — the group's members get the MSP role. Under **Applies to** you choose **The distributor's entire customer tree** (distributors only), **One provider's customers**, **Selected customers** or **Provider administration only**. "With selected customers, only at those, and only while the tenant reaches them."
- **Tenant role: Admin** — "Everyone in the group administers the tenant for as long as they are in it." The role goes when the person leaves the group, and a role set by hand is not affected. The members are marked **Admin via Entra**.

**Tenant role: Admin** needs the tenant to have a recovery administrator. Without one it says "Tenant role: Admin (needs a recovery administrator)", and the option can't be chosen. Appoint a recovery administrator first as described above.

If Entra turns an account off, the member is marked **Turned off by Entra**. See [Roles and permissions](/en/partner/roles-and-permissions).

### When SCIM is turned off

If you turn SCIM off, what SCIM gave stays. The page shows "SCIM is off, but what SCIM gave is still there" and how many members are affected.

Select **Revoke what SCIM gave** and confirm "Revoke everything SCIM gave in the tenant?". "Admin through Entra and the groups' assignments are removed. Manual roles and assignments are not affected. If you turn SCIM on again, the groups give back what they map."

## Sign-in on the tenant's own domain

If the tenant has its own domain under **Domain**, the **Signing in on the domain** card shows who signs in there:

- **Provider:** "Your staff, and your customers' users when the account reaches only your customers."
- **Distributor:** "Your staff, staff of providers you both administer and reach the customers of, and users at the customers you reach."

"Everyone else is signed in at {address} instead, and told why." How the domain is set up is described in [Custom domain](/en/organization/custom-domain), and what users see in [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in).
