Your AI clients' connections are under **My account** → **AI connections**, in the **Connected AI clients** section. Each connection is one approval. The same AI client can have several, for example for different customers.

## What you see

- **The client** and where the response is sent. **The name is reported by the client itself** means the client has given the name itself.
- **The state:**
  - **Waiting for the client**: approved, but the client has not collected the connection yet.
  - **Active**: in use, or ready for its first call.
  - **Expired** or **Inactive**: it has passed its 30 days or has not been used for 14 days.
  - **Ended**: you, an administrator, the client or an event has ended it. The reason is shown with the connection.
  - **Never collected**: you approved, but the client never collected the connection. Start over from the client.
  - **The sign-in no longer holds**, **Needs two-step verification** or **The account is not active**: the connection cannot be used until that is resolved. The explanation is shown with the connection.
- When it was approved and how you signed in, and **Verified: first call** once the client has made its first call.
- **Valid until** a date, and when it ends if it is not used.
- **The customers**, with the permissions the connection has at each one and a state per customer. If a customer has turned off AI clients, or your permissions at the customer have changed, the customer is shown as dormant with an explanation.

Ended connections are shown under **Ended in the last 30 days**.

## Add a customer

1. Click **Add customer** on the connection.
2. Confirm your sign-in if the portal asks for it.
3. Choose customers and permissions and click **Add**. A connection can reach at most 50 customers.

The client reaches the customer from its next call, without signing in again. The connection's validity does not change.

::note
You can only add customers you reach through the same sign-in the connection was approved with. If you approved the connection with a partner's SSO, for example, it reaches only that partner's customers. A customer that requires a different sign-in or role needs a new connection: connect the client again.
::

## Remove a customer

Click **Remove the customer**. The client no longer reaches the customer from the next call. The connection's other customers keep working.

## End a connection

Click **End the connection** and confirm. The client loses access to all of the connection's customers from the next call and must be connected again.

## When a connection ends by itself

A connection ends automatically when:

- your password is changed or reset;
- your two-step verification is changed or reset;
- your account is disabled;
- the sign-in service (SSO) you approved with is changed;
- the same refresh key is used twice, which can mean it has been copied;
- it has passed its 30 days or has not been used for 14 days.

What you do in the client itself, for example signing out, can also end the connection.
