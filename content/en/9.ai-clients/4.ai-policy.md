# AI policy

Under **Settings** → **Services** → **AI** the organization's administrator decides what AI clients and agents may do in the organization. The **Access** tab holds the policy, **Connections** shows who reaches the organization, and **Activity** shows what they have done.

::note
To change the AI policy you need the **Organization administration** permission. Every change is logged.
::

## How the AI policy applies

At the top the policy is summed up in a few sentences: who may connect, what they may read and propose, who approves changes and which AI clients may be used. The summary shows what actually holds, including what you inherit from your provider.

## Inheritance and locks

Your provider can give you a default and can lock a setting. A locked setting shows who locked it and cannot be changed here. A provider can tighten and forbid, but never force on you something that opens more.

## Who may connect

- **Personal connections.** People may connect AI clients that reach the organization on their behalf. Each person can only give their AI client what they hold themselves.
- **The organization's connections.** MCP keys and agents with a mandate of their own may be used, see [Autonomous agents](/en/ai-clients/autonomous-agents) and [MCP key](/en/ai-clients/mcp-key).

Both are off until they are turned on. The profiles below never change these choices.

## Profile

A profile fills in reading, proposing and approval at once:

- **Read only.** AI clients read, but propose no changes.
- **Propose changes.** AI clients propose new and changed records, and an authorized user approves.
- **Two persons.** Every change is approved by a verified person other than the one who took the initiative.
- **Provider managed.** Every change is approved by the organization's provider.

If you change something under **Customize**, the profile shows as custom.

## Approval

A proposal from an AI client changes nothing until someone approves it under **Proposals** in the module, for example **Cloudflare DNS** → **Proposals**. Whoever approves must themselves be allowed to make the change.

- **Ordinary changes** are approved by an authorized user, unless you require more.
- **Sensitive changes** require more: a verified person other than the initiator, or the customer's provider. Sensitive DNS changes are MX, NS, CAA, SPF, DMARC, DKIM and every record at the zone's top.

The proposal card shows who took the initiative, whether the change is sensitive and who must approve it.

## Let AI write directly

::warning
This choice is marked **Risky**. When it is on, an ordinary change an AI client proposes is carried out at once, without anyone in the portal approving it. A mistake, or a manipulated AI, can then change your records before anyone can stop it.
::

- Ordinary changes only. Sensitive changes are always approved by a person, and deletion is off.
- If you require approval of ordinary changes, by another person or by the provider for example, that requirement holds and nothing is carried out directly.
- Everything else is checked as usual: the connection's permission, protected records, and that the record has not changed since the proposal. All of it shows under **Activity**, and the proposal card shows **Carried out directly under the customer's policy, without approval**.
- Only you can turn it on. Your provider can lock it off.

Click **Turn on…**, tick that you understand what it means and click **Turn direct writing on**. You need to have signed in within the last 10 minutes; otherwise the portal asks you to confirm your sign-in. Turn it off with **Turn direct writing off**.

## Customize

Under **Customize** you set each part on its own:

- **Read.** One row per module with AI features. Search for a module and filter on **May be read**, **Locked** or **Own choice**. A module with several categories opens them in a drawer. The **Propose** and **Write directly** columns show the general choice for modules that can change something.
- **Propose.** Whether new and changed records may be proposed. Proposing deletion is locked by the platform.
- **Approval, every change** and **Approval, sensitive changes.** Which requirements hold: not whoever gave the connection its permission, another verified person, the provider, or another verified person or the provider.
- **AI clients.** Whether only AI clients in CloudPortal's registry of approved clients may be used.

Each row can **Follow the level above**, that is take what your provider chose, or have a choice of your own.

## AI usage policy

You can publish a text on how AI may be used with you. If it is required, an AI connection works with you only once the text is accepted. Personal connections are accepted by the person themselves, when they connect or under **My account** → **AI connections**. Keys and agents are accepted by an administrator under **Waiting for your acceptance**. A changed text is published as a new version, and the earlier ones remain.

## Connections and activity

- **Connections** shows every AI client and agent that reaches the organization: whose it is, which client, the path by which the person reaches you, what it may do, when it is valid until and when it was last used. Click **Revoke here** to close a connection with you from the next call.
- **Activity** shows the AI connections' calls and proposals: what was called, against which resource and with what outcome. The content of responses is never stored.
