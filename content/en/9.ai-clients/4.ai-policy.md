# AI policy

Under **Settings** → **Services** → **AI** the organization's administrator decides whether AI clients may reach the organization, and sees which ones do.

::note
To change the AI policy and revoke connections here, you need the **Organization administration** permission.
::

## The policy

Everything is off until an administrator turns it on, and every change is logged.

- **Allow members to connect their own AI clients over OAuth.** Each person connects for themselves and can only give their AI client what they hold themselves. If it is turned off, all such connections stop reaching the organization immediately.
- **Allow whoever gave a connection its permission to also approve its proposals.** For organizations with a single administrator. Otherwise a second person is always required, the so-called four-eyes principle. Self-approvals are marked in the log.

## Connections to the organization

Under **Connections to the organization** you see every AI client and agent that reaches the organization: whose it is, which client, the path by which the person reaches you, which permissions it has, when it is valid until and when it was last used.

Connections approved through the CloudPortal sign-in are marked **Connected through the CloudPortal sign-in**.

## Revoke a connection

Click **Revoke here**. The connection no longer reaches the organization from the next call. If the person gave the same connection other customers, those keep working, since they are not yours to end.
