# AI clients and MCP

You can connect your AI assistant, for example Claude, to CloudPortal. The connection uses **MCP** (Model Context Protocol), the open standard AI clients use to call tools.

A connected assistant can, for example, list a customer's DNS zones and records and, if you allow it, propose a change that a person then approves in the portal.

## How it works

1. You add the portal's address in your AI client.
2. The client sends you to CloudPortal. There you sign in as usual, choose customers and permissions, and approve.
3. When the client makes its first call, the portal shows it. That is your receipt that the connection works.

Nobody needs to administer anything in Microsoft Entra or any other service. Read more in [Connect your AI client](/en/ai-clients/connect-ai-client).

## This always applies

- **Only the customers you choose.** A connection reaches the customers you chose when you approved it. Every call names the customer, and customers are never added automatically.
- **Never more than you have.** You can only give the connection permissions you hold at the customer yourself, and if you lose a permission, the connection loses it too.
- **Read and propose, a person approves.** An assistant can propose a change, but nothing changes until someone with permission has approved the proposal in the portal. The customer decides how strict: another person, the provider, and for sensitive changes such as mail and delegation always more than one authorized user. A customer can also let AI write ordinary changes directly, a choice marked risky. See [AI policy](/en/ai-clients/ai-policy).
- **Everything is logged.** Every call shows in the customer's [audit log](/en/organization/audit-logs), with who called and through which client.
- **Off until the customer turns it on.** A customer is reached only if its administrator has allowed AI clients in the [AI policy](/en/ai-clients/ai-policy).
- **Limited time.** A connection lasts 30 days from your sign-in and ends earlier if it is not used for 14 days.

## What an assistant can do

It depends on the permissions you gave the connection. Typical tools:

- Which customers the connection reaches, and **What this connection may do** at a customer.
- **List Cloudflare zones** and **List DNS records in a zone**.
- **Propose a DNS record change**. The proposal is approved in the portal under **Cloudflare DNS** → **Proposals**.
- **Status of a proposal**: whether it is waiting, carried out, rejected or in conflict.
