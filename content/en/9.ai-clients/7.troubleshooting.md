# Troubleshooting

## The page says "The connection expired"

The approval must happen within 15 minutes and in the same browser the AI client opened. Start the connection over from the AI client. If someone else sent you the link to the page, it does not work for you: that protects you from being tricked into approving someone else's client.

## The page asks me to "Confirm your sign-in"

You must have signed in within the last 10 minutes to approve.

- **With a password:** click **Confirm with password** and enter your password, and the code if you use two-step verification.
- **With SSO:** click **Sign in again**. You are sent to your sign-in service and back.

If the page then says that **the sign-in service did not confirm a new sign-in**, your sign-in service does not state the sign-in time. Whoever administers the sign-in needs to make the service state it (`auth_time`).

## A customer cannot be chosen

- **AI clients are turned off at the customer.** An administrator at the customer can turn them on in the [AI policy](/en/ai-clients/ai-policy).
- **The customer does not show at all.** If you signed in with a password, customers at partners that require SSO are not shown. Sign in with SSO and connect again.

## "Add customer" says the customer requires a new connection

The connection reaches customers only through the same sign-in it was approved with. Connect the client again and choose the customer then.

## The client says it cannot reach the customer

Check the customer's state under **My account** → **AI connections**. If it is **dormant**, the reason is shown there: the customer may have turned off AI clients, or your permissions at the customer have changed.

## The connection has ended

The reason is shown under **Ended in the last 30 days**. A password change or a changed two-step verification ends all your connections. Connect the client again. Read more in [Manage your connections](/en/ai-clients/manage-connections).
