# Organization & Teams

Under **Settings** you manage your organization: who has access, what they can do, which modules are shown and how the portal looks. The settings require the **Organization administration** permission.

## The overview

The start page of Settings shows the organization and your role in it. Under **Common tasks** there are shortcuts to what you do most often:

- **Invite member** — see [Members](/en/organization/members).
- **Invite guest** — see [External guests](/en/organization/external-guests).
- **Create API key** — see [API keys](/en/organization/api-tokens).

You only see the tasks you have permission for.

## The menu

Every page is in the menu on the left.

**Access**

- **Members** — invite people, change roles and remove access. Here you also see your provider's access and [external guests](/en/organization/external-guests). See [Members](/en/organization/members) and [Roles and permissions](/en/organization/roles-and-permissions).
- **Groups** and **Group permissions** — give several people the same module permissions. See [Groups](/en/organization/groups).
- **Delegations** — lend permissions to a person at your provider for a limited time. See [Delegations](/en/organization/delegations).
- **Auth & SSO** — sign-in through your identity provider and automatic account management. See [Single sign-on (SSO)](/en/organization/sso) and [SCIM provisioning](/en/organization/scim).
- **API keys** — keys for integrations with the modules' API. See [API keys](/en/organization/api-tokens).

**Services**

- **Modules** — choose which modules are active in the organization. See [Modules](/en/organization/modules).
- **Email** — send the portal's email from your own domain and follow what is sent. See [Email](/en/organization/email).

**Appearance**

- **Branding** — logo, symbol, colors and the sign-in page. See [Branding](/en/organization/branding).
- **Domain** — reach the portal and sign in on your own domain. See [Custom domain](/en/organization/custom-domain).

**Operations & audit**

- **Operations** — operational notices to the organization. See [Service status](/en/support/service-status).
- **Logs** — track which changes were made and by whom. See [Audit logs](/en/organization/audit-logs).
