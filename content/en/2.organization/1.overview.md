# Organization & Teams

Under organization settings you manage who has access to your organization and what they can do.

## What you can do

- **Members** — invite people, remove access and see who has access, including at your provider.
- **Roles and permissions** — control what different members can see and do.
- **Delegations** — lend permissions to a person at your provider for a limited time.
- **Single sign-on (SSO)** — let users sign in with your identity provider.
- **SCIM provisioning** — create users and assign roles automatically via groups.
- **Custom domain** — reach the portal on your own domain with automatic SSL.
- **Branding** — customize the logo and colors.
- **Audit logs** — track which changes were made and by whom.
- **API keys** — create keys with permissions and an end date for integrations with the modules' API.
