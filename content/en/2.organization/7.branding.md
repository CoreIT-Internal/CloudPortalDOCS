# Branding

You customize the portal's look to match your brand on the **Branding & login** page under **Settings** → **Appearance** → **Branding**.

If you have no logo or color of your own, the provider's or distributor's is used, and otherwise the portal's default.

::note
If you are a provider or distributor, **Branding** in the partner area applies to your own tenant. A customer's branding is set in the customer's own settings.
::

## Logo

Under **App branding** you control the logo shown in the navigation throughout the portal.

- **Change logo** — upload JPG, PNG, WebP or SVG, at most 2 MB.
- **Remove logo** — the portal then uses the inherited logo or the default logo.

**Logo source** shows where the logo comes from, for example **Organization** or **Provider**.

## Symbol

The symbol is "the square image shown for the organization in the organization switcher, on the Choose organization page and in the tenant tree". The logo is made for the navigation bar and becomes unreadable in such a small box.

- Use PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG, roughly square, at least 64 × 64 px (not for SVG) and at most 2 MB.
- Choose **Upload symbol**, **Replace symbol** or **Remove symbol**.

The symbol is never inherited from the provider. Without a symbol, the organization's initials are shown.

## Colors

You can change two colors:

- **Accent color** — used for buttons, links and highlights in the portal.
- **Navigation** — the background color of the main menu.

Choose with the color picker, type a color code such as `#1A2B3C`, or click one of the suggestions. **Use the provider's colour** removes your own color.

Changes are a draft until you save. The preview next to them is marked "Preview – not saved" and shows how the portal looks with the new colors. Nothing changes for anyone until you save.

1. Click **Save…**.
2. The **Save the colours for {organization}?** box shows what changes.
3. Click **Save for everyone**. The colors apply to everyone in the organization at once.

With **Undo changes** you go back to what is saved. If you try to leave the page with unsaved changes, the portal asks **Leave without saving?**.

::tip
The text on buttons and other surfaces in the accent color automatically becomes white or black, so that it is always readable.
::

## Email logo

Under **Email branding** you upload a separate logo for the portal's email. Use PNG or JPG, at most 2 MB — they are shown in every email client, Outlook included. Without an email logo of your own, the logo from **App branding** is used.

## The sign-in page

Under **Login branding** you customize the sign-in page:

- **Logo (light background)** — shown when the user has a light theme.
- **Logo (dark background)** — shown when the user has a dark theme.
- **Background image** — an optional image behind the sign-in form.

The logos may be at most 2 MB and the background image at most 5 MB, in JPG, PNG, WebP or SVG. With **Change file** and **Remove** you replace or remove an image. If you remove an image, the sign-in page shows the inherited image or the default image instead.

**Panel background color** sets the color behind the sign-in form. When there is a background image, the color is laid over it, and with **Intensity** you control how strong it is. Click **Save**, or **Reset** to go back to the default color.

The sign-in page shows your brand on your own domain when sign-in is available there. Use the **See the domain's status** link to check. Read more under [Custom domain](/en/organization/custom-domain).
