# Members

On the **Members** page you see everyone who has access to your organization, which role they have and whether they are active. Here you invite new people, change their role and remove access. You find the page under **Settings** → **Access** → **Members**.

::note
To invite or manage members you need permission to do so. Without it, an information box is shown and the buttons are disabled. Ask an administrator or owner for help.
::

## The member list

The list has the columns:

- **Person** — name and email. The **You** badge marks yourself, and the **MFA** badge means "Two-step verification is on". An icon shows that the person has manually customized permissions on top of their role.
- **Role** — controls what the person may do (see [Roles and permissions](/en/organization/roles-and-permissions)). For an external guest it says **External guest** and how long the access lasts.
- **Status** — **Active**, **Invited**, **Terminated** or **Ended**.
- **Source** — how the person got in: **Added manually**, **SCIM** or **Created at sign-in (JIT)**. For a guest it shows the sponsor, for example "Sponsor: Anna Andersson".
- **Actions** — what you may do with the person.

At the top the situation is summarized, for example "12 active · 2 invited · 1 pending invitations".

::tip
Use the search field to find a person by name or email. Tick **Show only members with manual permissions** to see who has customized permissions on top of their role.
::

## Inviting a member

1. Click **Invite member** in the top right.
2. Enter the person's **Email address**.
3. Choose a **Role**. The roles are shown as cards with a description, and **Member** is preselected:
   - **Owner** — "The highest role. Only an owner can appoint or remove an owner – except when the organization has none: then an administrator can appoint the first."
   - **Admin** — "Administers the organization: settings, members and permissions."
   - **Operator** — "Does the operational work in the modules and sees who the members are."
   - **Member** — "Works in the modules within the default permissions and sees who the members are."
   - **Viewer** — "Reads. Does not see the member list."
   - **Support** — "Helps out: sees members and the organization's log, changes nothing."
4. Click **Send invitation**.

The role can be changed later. You fine-tune individual module permissions with **Module permissions** on the member's row (see [Roles and permissions](/en/organization/roles-and-permissions)).

You can only hand out roles you are allowed to give yourself. The other roles are shown locked with the text "You cannot hand out this role." If you are not an owner, **Owner** is locked with the text "Only an owner can appoint an owner."

::note
If the organization requires SSO and the address is outside the organization's verified domains, a warning is shown: "The organization requires SSO, and the address is outside its verified domains. The person gets in only if the organization's sign-in recognizes them."
::

The person gets an email and becomes a member when the invitation is accepted. This also applies to someone who already has an account in the portal. The invitation is shown under **Pending invitations** until it is accepted.

Once the invitation has been accepted, you as the inviter get an email, "Invitation accepted", as long as you may still see the organization's members.

If there already is an invitation to the address, you see "There is already a pending invitation to {email}. You can send it again instead of creating a new one."

To give someone outside the organization and the provider time-limited access, use **Give an external person access** instead. See [External guests](/en/organization/external-guests).

### When the email does not go out at once

If the email provider does not respond, the message is queued: "The invitation to {email} is saved. The email is queued and will be sent as soon as the email provider responds." You don't need to do anything. The email is shown under [Email](/en/organization/email#outbox).

If the message could not be sent at all, you see "The invitation to {email} is saved, but the email could not be sent.", followed by the reason. The invitation then remains under **Pending invitations**, and you can try again with **Resend**.

### Managing pending invitations

Under **Pending invitations** each invitation shows how long it is valid and who invited, for example "valid until Nov 15, 2026 · invited by Anna Andersson". You can:

- **Resend** — if the email didn't arrive. If the new send fails, the earlier invitation link still works.
- **Renew and send** — for an invitation that has expired ("expired {date}"). The invitation stays in the list, and you renew it instead of creating a new one.
- **Cancel** — to withdraw the invitation before it is accepted. The link that was sent stops working.

## Changing a member's role

1. Find the member in the list.
2. Choose a new role in the drop-down in the **Role** column.

The change is saved immediately. Only active members can change role, and the menu only shows the roles you may give. The same owner rule applies here: only an owner can make someone an owner, except when the organization has no owner.

Some changes ask first:

- **Raise the role?** — when you give someone else a higher role.
- **Stop being an owner?** — when you leave the owner role yourself. Only another owner can give the role back to you.

::warning
Be careful when lowering your own permissions. If a change would remove capabilities you need, a confirmation box describes what you would lose. Make sure someone else keeps full administrative access before you confirm.
::

## Disabling or re-enabling access

- **Disable** — pauses an active member's access. The portal asks "Deactivate the member?". The person cannot use the organization, but role and settings are kept. The status becomes **Terminated**.
- **Enable** — restores the access.

This is useful for example during parental leave or temporary assignments.

## Removing a member

1. Click **Remove permanently** on the member's row.
2. Confirm in the **Remove the member?** box.

The person is removed from the organization, with their group memberships and own module permissions. They have to be invited again to come back.

::warning
If the member comes from your company's automatic account sync (source **SCIM**), the person may be added back automatically at the next sync. In that case, turn off the access in your sign-in system to prevent it.
::

## Resetting two-step verification

**Reset MFA** is only shown to administrators at your provider, and only on rows where two-step verification is on. The reset signs the person out everywhere, and the app and backup codes stop working. At the next sign-in the person sets up two-step verification again where it is required.

If a member has lost both their phone and their backup codes, contact your provider.

## Your provider's access

Further down the page, the **Your provider's access** panel shows which people at your provider (or a distributor serving you) reach the organization through an MSP role.

For each person you see:

- name and email
- which provider the person belongs to
- the role and its scope: "all of the provider's customers", "the distributor's whole customer tree" or "selected organizations"

Click **Show permissions** to see what the role grants. Sensitive permissions are marked.

The panel is read-only — your provider manages its own staff. If someone at the provider needs more access for a short time, you lend it with a [delegation](/en/organization/delegations). People who have access through a delegation are shown under **Delegated access**.
