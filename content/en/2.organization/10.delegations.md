# Delegations

A delegation lends some of the organization's module permissions, for a limited time, to a person at your provider (MSP). The person does not become a member and does not see your settings or your member list.

Examples of when a delegation fits:

- the provider's consultant needs to change DNS records during a migration
- support needs to troubleshoot certificates for a week
- an existing consultant role needs one more permission, without changing the role

You find the page under **Settings** → **Access** → **Delegations**.

::note
To see and manage delegations you need the **Organization administration** permission. If you have no permissions that can be lent, you see "You cannot lend permissions here".
::

## Rules

- A delegation can only go to someone at a provider that already serves the organization.
- It always ends on its end date, at most 90 days ahead.
- Sensitive permissions cannot be lent out, and you can only lend what you hold yourself.
- An external person — for example a consultant with no link to your provider — gets time-limited guest access with selected permissions instead. Use the **Give an external person access →** link, or see [External guests](/en/organization/external-guests).

## Creating a delegation

The **New delegation** form has three steps: **Who**, **What** and **How long**.

### 1. Who

Enter **Email address of the person at the provider** and click **Check**.

- If the person can receive a delegation, their name and "Works at {provider}" are shown.
- If the person cannot receive a delegation, "Cannot receive a delegation" is shown. Check the spelling. If it is an external person, use the **Give an external person access →** link.

You may also get a warning:

- "The person is already a member of the organization. Consider changing their role instead."
- "The person already has an active delegation. The new one replaces it."

### 2. What

Choose permissions per module. The permissions are shown by name, and you can search modules and permissions or use **Select all** per module. Only permissions you can lend are offered. The module instead says, for example, "2 permissions in the module cannot be lent by you (sensitive or not yours)."

### 3. How long

Choose **Valid through**: 7, 30 or 90 days, or a date of your own. 30 days is preselected. The field is required, at most 90 days ahead, and the delegation ends at the end of the day.

Optionally fill in **Reason (optional)**, for example a ticket number.

### Create

Before you create the delegation a summary is shown, for example "Anna Andersson (Provider Ltd) gets 5 permissions in the organization through Oct 30, 2026." Click **Create delegation**.

## Viewing and revoking delegations

The **Delegations** list is grouped per person and shows how many are in force. For each delegation it shows status, end date and any reason:

- **In force** — the delegation applies.
- **Not in force** — the delegation has not expired but no longer applies. The reason is shown next to it, for example "The person no longer works at the provider."
- **Expired** — the end date has passed.
- **Revoked** — the delegation has been revoked.

You can:

- **Show {count} permissions** — see which permissions the delegation grants.
- **Show revoked** — include revoked delegations in the list.
- **Revoke** — the portal asks **Revoke the delegation?**: "The delegation to {name} ends at once. If the person has access in another way, for example through an MSP role, that remains."

::tip
Give a delegation as short a time as the work requires. If the person needs to continue after the end date, create a new delegation. If the person already has an active delegation, the new one replaces the old one.
::
