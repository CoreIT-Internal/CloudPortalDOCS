# Delegations

A delegation lends some of the organization's module permissions, for a limited time, to a person at your provider (MSP). The person does not become a member and does not see your settings or your member list.

Examples of when a delegation fits:

- the provider's consultant needs to change DNS records during a migration
- support needs to troubleshoot certificates for a week
- an existing consultant role needs one more permission, without changing the role

You find the page under **Settings** → **Delegations** (the same page is also reached via **Members & access** → **Delegations**).

::note
To see and manage delegations you need the **Organization administration** permission.
::

## Rules

- A delegation can only go to someone at a provider that already serves the organization.
- It always ends on its end date, at most 90 days ahead.
- Sensitive permissions cannot be lent out.
- External people — for example a consultant with no connection to your provider — are invited as [members](/en/organization/members) instead.

## Creating a delegation

The **New delegation** form has three steps: **Who**, **What** and **How long**.

### 1. Who

Enter the **Email address of the person at the provider** and click **Check**.

- If the person can receive a delegation, their name and "Works at {provider}" are shown.
- If the person cannot receive a delegation, "Cannot receive a delegation" is shown. Check the spelling, or use the **Invite as member →** link.

You may also get a warning:

- "The person is already a member of the organization. Consider changing their role instead."
- "The person already has an active delegation. The new one replaces it."

### 2. What

Choose permissions per module. The permissions are shown by name, and you can search modules and permissions or use **Select all** per module. Sensitive permissions are not offered as choices; instead the module states, for example, "2 sensitive permissions in the module cannot be lent out."

### 3. How long

Choose **Valid through**: 7, 30 or 90 days, or a date of your own. 30 days is preselected. The field is required, at most 90 days ahead, and the delegation ends at the end of the day.

Optionally fill in **Reason (optional)**, for example a ticket number.

### Create

Before you create the delegation, a summary is shown, for example "Anna Andersson (Provider Ltd) gets 5 permissions in the organization through 30 Oct 2026." Click **Create delegation**.

## Viewing and revoking delegations

The **Active delegations** list is grouped by person. For each delegation it shows the status (active, expired or revoked), the end date and any reason.

- **Show permissions** — shows which permissions the delegation grants.
- **Show revoked** — includes revoked delegations in the list.
- **Revoke** — the button changes to **Click again to revoke**. Click once more to confirm.

::tip
Give a delegation as little time as the work requires. If the person needs to continue after the end date, create a new delegation. If the person already has an active delegation, the new one replaces the old one.
::
