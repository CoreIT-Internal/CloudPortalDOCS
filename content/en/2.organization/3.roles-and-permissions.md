# Roles and permissions

Every member of the organization has a **role**. The role decides what the person can see and do. You assign roles on the [Members](/en/organization/members) page.

## The roles at a glance

| Role | Description in the portal |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The highest role. Only an owner can appoint or remove an owner – except when the organization has none: then an administrator can appoint the first. |
| **Admin** | Administers the organization: settings, members and permissions. |
| **Operator** | Does the operational work in the modules and sees who the members are. |
| **Member** | Works in the modules within the default permissions and sees who the members are. |
| **Viewer** | Reads. Does not see the member list. |
| **Support** | Helps out: sees members and the organization's log, changes nothing. |

::note
Exactly what each role can do also depends on which modules and features are enabled in your organization. The table describes the usual outcome.
::

### The owner rule

Only an owner can appoint a new owner or remove an owner. The exception is an organization without an owner — then an administrator can appoint the first. For everyone else **Owner** is locked, both when you invite and in the role picker in the member list.

## How to assign a role

1. Go to [Members](/en/organization/members).
2. Find the person in the list.
3. Choose the role in the drop-down in the **Role** column.

The change is saved immediately.

::tip
Give each person the lowest role that is enough for their work. It is easier to raise permissions later than to untangle rights that are too broad.
::

## Custom permissions per member

The role gives the base permissions. On top of it, you can fine-tune what an individual member may do in the modules. Click **Module permissions** on the member's row. A panel opens with the organization's modules.

For each permission you choose:

- **Allow** — give the member the permission.
- **Deny** — block the permission even if the role would normally grant it.
- **Inherit** — follow what the role grants (no special setting).

Each permission shows whether the person **Has the permission now** or **Does not have the permission now**. For a whole module you can choose **Allow all**, **Deny all** or **Inherit all from the role**.

::note
A permission marked **Blocked by the module policy** cannot be given, because the organization's module settings stop it.
::

Changes are marked **Unsaved**. Click **Save** for a module, or **Save all** to save everything at once. Members with custom permissions get a marker in the member list and can be filtered there.

::warning
Custom permissions sit on top of the role and are easy to forget. Use them sparingly and review them regularly, so that access stays predictable.
::

## Permissions through groups

If several people should have the same module permissions, gather them in a group. The members get what the group gives, on top of their role. See [Groups](/en/organization/groups).

## External guests

An external guest has no role and cannot be put in groups. The guest only gets the permissions you chose when you invited them, for a limited time. See [External guests](/en/organization/external-guests).

## Access outside membership

Besides your own members and guests, people at your provider can reach the organization in two ways:

- **MSP role** — the provider's staff can reach the organization through an MSP role the provider assigns. You see who they are and what the role grants on the [Members](/en/organization/members) page, in the **Your provider's access** panel.
- **Delegation** — you can lend some of the organization's module permissions yourselves, for a limited time, to a person at the provider. See [Delegations](/en/organization/delegations).

::note
A role at the provider does not by itself give any access into your organization. Access requires a membership, an MSP assignment or a delegation.
::
