# SCIM provisioning

SCIM lets your identity provider (e.g. Microsoft Entra ID) **create and update users automatically** in CloudPortal and **assign roles via groups**. SSO handles signing in — SCIM handles which accounts exist and what they can do.

::note
SCIM requires [SSO](/en/organization/sso) to be set up first. The **SCIM Provisioning** section appears under **Settings → Auth & SSO** once the organization has a connection to an identity provider. Providers and distributors configure SCIM for their own tenant under **Partner → Tenants → [tenant] → Auth & SSO**.
::

## Enable SCIM

1. Go to **Settings → Auth & SSO → SCIM Provisioning**.
2. Click **Generate new token**. The portal shows a **bearer token once** — copy it immediately (it can't be shown again).
3. Note the **Tenant URL** the portal displays:
   ```
   https://<your-domain>/api/scim/v2
   ```

## Connect it in your identity provider

In Entra, for example (Enterprise Application → Provisioning):

1. Set **Provisioning Mode** to *Automatic*.
2. Under **Admin Credentials**:
   - **Tenant URL** → paste `https://<your-domain>/api/scim/v2`
   - **Secret Token** → paste the token from the portal
3. Click **Test Connection** to verify.
4. Enable group provisioning and assign the users/groups to sync.
5. Start provisioning.

::note
The first sync can take a while (often around 40 minutes, depending on your provider). Synced groups appear in the portal but grant **no access** until you map them to roles (below).
::

## Map groups to roles

In the **Group-Role Mappings** section, link a synced group to permissions:

1. Click **Add mapping** and pick a group.
2. Choose the type:
   - **Organization role** — admin, operator, member, viewer or support
   - **Module bundle** — a combined level (e.g. Admin/Operator/Viewer) for a module
   - **Single permission (advanced)** — one individual permission in a module
   - **Provisioning** — presence only, no permissions
   - **MSP role** — only at provider and distributor level (see below)
3. Choose what happens **when a user is removed from the group**. The options depend on the type:
   - **Remove the grant** — available for every type, and recommended.
   - **Downgrade to the default role** — organization role only.
   - **No action (provisioning only)** — provisioning only.
4. Click **Add**. Users in the group get the role **immediately** — no re-sync needed.

### MSP role: choose where the role applies

A mapping to an **MSP role** requires you to choose where it **applies to**:

- **One provider's customers** — the role applies at a provider's customers. If you are a distributor, you also choose which provider.
- **The distributor's entire customer tree** — the role applies at all customers in the distributor's tree. Distributors only.

Members of the group get the role at the customers in the selected area. Individually selected organizations are assigned in the portal, not via SCIM. Read more about MSP roles and customer access under [Partner roles and permissions](/en/partner/roles-and-permissions).

::tip
If you map an **organization role** at provider or distributor level, you can tick **Applies to all child organizations**. The role then takes effect in every organization under the tenant. The option is only available for organization roles.
::

## Manage tokens

The token list shows prefix, created date, last used and status. You can **revoke** individual tokens, or **disable SCIM** entirely (which revokes all tokens).
