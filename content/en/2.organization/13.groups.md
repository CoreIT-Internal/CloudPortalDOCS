# Groups

"A group gathers people who should have the same module permissions. Members get what the group gives, on top of their role." You manage the groups under **Settings** → **Access** → **Groups**, and what they give under **Group permissions**.

Changes are checked as grants: you can only give what you hold yourself.

::note
External guests cannot be put in groups. See [External guests](/en/organization/external-guests).
::

## Creating a group

1. Go to **Settings** → **Access** → **Groups**.
2. Click **Create group**.
3. Enter a **Name** and an optional **Description**.
4. Click **Create group**.

Then add members and permissions.

## Where groups come from

The **Existing groups** list shows, for each group, the number of members, what it gives and where it comes from:

- **Created in the portal** — you manage name, description and members here.
- **From SCIM** — the group comes from your identity provider. Its name and members are changed there. Here you can change its description and permissions. See [SCIM provisioning](/en/organization/scim).

## Managing members

Click a group to open it.

1. Search for a person under **Add member** and click **Add**. Only active members of the organization can be added.
2. Remove a person with **Remove** on their row.
3. Click **Save members**. With **Undo changes** you go back to what is saved.

If a SCIM mapping gives the group's members a role, it says: "Adding or removing someone changes their role too."

With **Delete group** you delete a group created in the portal. Its members lose the permissions and roles the group gave them.

## Group permissions

On the **Group Permissions** page you set what each group gives. You reach it through the menu or with **Edit the group's permissions →** in an open group.

1. Click the group and then a module.
2. For each permission you choose:
   - **Grant** — the group's members get the permission.
   - **Deny** — the group's members are blocked from the permission.
   - Neither (inherit) — no special setting. Click the selected option again to remove it.
3. With **Grant all**, **Deny all** and **Clear** you set a whole module at once.
4. Click **Save permissions**. The number of unsaved changes is shown next to the button.

You can search the modules and tick **Show only enabled modules**. A module that is not active in the organization is marked **Not activated**.

::note
"The organization's module policy always comes first: what it blocks, no group can give." A denied permission in a group only limits what the member gets through their membership, not access through the provider or a delegation.
::
