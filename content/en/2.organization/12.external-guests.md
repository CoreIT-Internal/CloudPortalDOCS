# External guests

An external guest is a consultant or partner outside the organization and the provider. The guest only gets the permissions you choose, and the access ends by itself.

- A guest gets no role and cannot be put in groups.
- You can only give permissions you hold yourself and that may be lent.
- The access lasts at most 365 days.

::note
If it is a person at your provider, use a [delegation](/en/organization/delegations) instead. If the person should become a regular member, invite them as a [member](/en/organization/members).
::

## Inviting a guest

Go to **Settings** → **Access** → **Members** and click **Give an external person access**. You reach the same guide through **Invite guest** on the Settings overview. The guide has four steps.

### 1. Email

Enter the person's **Email address**.

### 2. Access

Choose the individual permissions the guest should get. Only permissions you hold and that may be lent are shown. If you have none, it says "You hold no permissions that can be given to a guest here."

### 3. Validity

- **Valid until** — choose 7, 30, 90 or 365 days, or a date of your own. 30 days is preselected, and the access lasts at most 365 days. It ends at the end of the day.
- **Sponsor** — a member of the organization who is responsible for the guest. You are preselected, and you switch with **Change**. If the sponsor leaves the organization, the access cannot be extended until a new one has been chosen.
- **Purpose** — for example "DNS migration, ticket 1234". At least 3 characters.

Sponsor and purpose are required.

### 4. Review and send

The summary shows exactly what is given: organization, recipient, permissions, end date, sponsor and purpose. "The guest gets no other permissions, no role and no group. The access ends at the end date."

Click **Send invitation**. The invitation is shown under **Pending invitations** on the Members page, marked **External guest**.

::note
If something changes before the invitation is accepted, for example that you no longer hold one of the permissions yourself, the invitation stops rather than giving something else. The guest then sees "The invitation cannot be accepted as it stands. Ask the organization for a new one."
::

## What the guest sees

The guest gets an email with a link. The invitation page says "You are invited as an external guest", how long the access lasts, the purpose and which permissions are included. The guest accepts with an existing account or creates a new one.

When the guest selects an organization, it is shown marked as guest, with the end date. See [Select organization](/en/getting-started/select-organization).

## Guests in the member list

In the member list, the **Role** column says **External guest**, with the end date. The **Source** column shows the sponsor, for example "Sponsor: Anna Andersson". When the access has ended, the status becomes **Ended**.

On the guest's row you can:

- **Extend** — choose a new end date while the access is valid. If you pick a date before the current one, the access is shortened instead. If the sponsor has left the organization, choose a new sponsor first.
- **Module permissions** — change the guest's permissions while the access is valid.
- **Reactivate** — give a guest whose access has ended access again. You go through the guide once more, without the email step. "Earlier permissions do not come back, only the selection below."
- **Remove permanently** — remove the guest from the organization.
