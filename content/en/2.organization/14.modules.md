# Modules

On the **Modules** page you see the state of each module in the organization and why it applies. You find the page under **Settings** → **Services** → **Modules**.

To change the state you need the **Organization administration** permission.

## States

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Listed, and usable by those with permission. |
| **Not activated** | Listed greyed out; cannot be opened. |
| **Hidden** | Not shown at all. |
| **Locked** | Decided above you; cannot be changed here. |

The state also controls what is shown on the [dashboard](/en/getting-started/dashboard): a module that is **Not activated** is shown greyed out, and a **Hidden** module is not shown at all.

## The list

The modules are grouped by category. The list has the columns:

- **Module** — name and a short description.
- **State** — what applies right now.
- **Why** — who decided the state:
  - **Own choice** — you chose it yourselves.
  - **Follows the provider’s default**, **Follows the distributor’s default** or **Follows the platform’s default** — you follow what was chosen above you.
  - For a locked module it says how and by whom, for example "Locked · the provider" or "Coming soon · the platform". Any message or reason is shown below.
- **Your choice** — where you change the state.

Filter with **All**, **Active**, **Not activated**, **Hidden**, **Locked** and **Own choice**. You can also search for a module and pick a category.

## Changing the state of a module

Choose in the **Your choice** column:

- **Follow the default ({state})** — follow what the provider, distributor or platform has chosen. If they change their choice, your state changes too.
- **Active**, **Not activated** or **Hidden** — your own choice, which applies until you change it.

The change is saved immediately. If the change takes an active module away, the portal asks first, for example **Change DNS to Hidden?**: "Nobody in {organization} can use DNS until it is activated again."

For a locked module it says "Cannot be changed here". Your own choice is saved and applies again when the lock is removed.

## Why the state applies

Click a module to open the details panel. There you can also change the state and click **Save**.

Under **Why this state applies** the choice is shown level by level: the organization, the provider, the distributor and the platform. "The nearest choice applies: your own, else the provider’s, the distributor’s and last the platform’s. A lock at any level comes before all of them."

The panel also shows which permissions the module has, with links to **Members** and **Group Permissions** where you grant them, and **Open the module** when it is active.
