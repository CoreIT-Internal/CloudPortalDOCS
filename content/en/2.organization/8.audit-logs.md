# Audit logs

The log shows a history of important events in your organization — for example when someone is invited, changes role or a setting is changed. It provides traceability over who did what and when. You find the **Log** page under **Settings** → **Operations & audit** → **Logs**.

::note
To view the log you need the permission **The organization's log**. Without it you see "The log is not available to you". Then contact an administrator or owner in the organization.
::

## Reading the log

Each row in the log shows:

- **Timestamp** — when the event occurred.
- **Event** — which type of event it concerns.
- **User** — who performed the action.
- **Details** — click **View** to see more information about an individual event.

Above the list it says which time zone the times are in, for example "Dates and times are in the Europe/Stockholm time zone."

The log is shown page by page, for example "Showing 1–25 of 340". Use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom to page through it.

## Filtering events

At the top there are filters to help you find the right events:

1. **Module** — limit to a certain part of the portal.
2. **Event type** — choose a specific type of event (the list adapts to the chosen module).
3. **From date** and **To date** — limit to a time period.

The log updates automatically when you change a filter. Click **Clear** to return to the default view.

::tip
By default the most recent events are shown. To look into something that happened a while ago, set **From date** and **To date** so they cover the right period.
::

## What the log shows and does not show

The log is meant to give insight into which actions have been performed. It lists events, identifiers and status — not the full content of what was changed.

::note
The log is meant for follow-up and review, not as a complete backup or restore tool. If you need to restore something, use the relevant feature in the portal.
::
