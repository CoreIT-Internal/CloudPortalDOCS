# Email Security

The email security module helps you publish and monitor standards that protect your email domain. The portal hosts what needs hosting and writes the DNS records for you when the domain's zone is in the portal.

## What you can do

- **[MTA-STS](/en/email-security/mta-sts)** — publish a policy that requires encrypted email delivery to your domain.
- **[Enforce and MX change](/en/email-security/enforce-and-mx-change)** — switch an MTA-STS policy to enforce after a precheck, and change MX without losing mail.
- **[BIMI](/en/email-security/bimi)** — display your logo in recipients' inboxes (requires DMARC in enforcement).
- **[Domain ownership and transfer](/en/email-security/domain-ownership-and-transfer)** — prove that the organization owns the domain, and move a domain between organizations.
- **[Lookup tool](/en/email-security/lookup-tool)** — check which records are published for a domain.

::warning
The portal publishes the DNS records for you, but receiving mail servers decide for themselves whether to honor them. "Published" means the record exists in DNS — not that every recipient respects it.
::

## Finding the features

Go to **Email security**. The overview shows the features as tiles. A tile marked **Coming soon** cannot be used yet. In the menu, **MTA-STS** and **BIMI** are under **Policies**, and **Lookup** is under **Tools**.

You can also reach email security from a zone in **DNS** or **Cloudflare DNS**. The zone header has the buttons:

- **Check email security** — opens the [Lookup tool](/en/email-security/lookup-tool) and runs a check of the domain.
- **MTA-STS for this domain** — opens the domain's MTA-STS policy.
- **BIMI for this domain** — opens the domain's BIMI policy.

If the domain has no policy yet, the page says "No policy found for …", and if you may create one, a button opens a new policy with the domain filled in.

In the **DNS** module, **MTA-STS for this domain** and **BIMI for this domain** are shown only to the zone's owner, not for a zone shared with you.

## Permissions

| Permission | Grants | Held by default by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **View email security policies** | See policies and use the lookup tool. | All roles |
| **Manage email security policies** | Create and change policies, upload BIMI files, verify ownership of a domain, delete policies, request a transfer and release a domain. | **Owner**, **Administrator**, **Operator** |
| **Publish email security policies** | Publish, decommission and unpublish, change MX in steps and stop serving an inherited policy. | **Owner**, **Administrator**, **Operator** |

For the portal to write the records in a zone, the person publishing also needs **Edit DNS records** (zones in **DNS**) or **Edit Cloudflare records** (zones in **Cloudflare DNS**). Without that right, the policy is published as customer-managed and the records are added by hand. Taking over records in the zone that the portal did not create always requires that right.

Read more about roles and custom permissions in [Roles and permissions](/en/organization/roles-and-permissions).

## Email notifications

The portal sends email when something needs action:

| Event | Recipients |
| --- | --- |
| A domain's MX points at hosts that a published MTA-STS policy in enforce does not cover. See [Enforce and MX change](/en/email-security/enforce-and-mx-change). | The organization's owners and administrators, and administrators at your provider. |
| Control of a domain could not be confirmed, and editing is frozen. See [Domain ownership and transfer](/en/email-security/domain-ownership-and-transfer). | The organization's owners and administrators, and administrators at your provider. |
| An MTA-STS publish stands still: the policy has waited a day for its certificate, or its DNS records could not be written for an hour. | The organization's owners and administrators, and administrators at your provider. |
| Another organization has requested a transfer of your domain, and when the transfer is completed, fails or is withdrawn. | Owners and administrators of the organization that owns the domain. |

Each event gives one email per recipient. The email links to the right page in the portal.
