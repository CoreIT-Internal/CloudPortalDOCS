# Dashboard

The dashboard is the home page after you sign in. From here you reach all of the portal's modules for the organization you're working in. You also see active incidents and the latest news here.

## The top bar

- **Search** — search for pages, modules and resources in the organization you work in. Open the search with **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac). Below the field it says "Searching in {organization}". Each result is marked with its type, for example **Page**, **Module**, **DNS zone**, **Certificate**, **Redirect**, **Application** or **Email policy**.
- **Documentation** — opens this documentation.
- **Support** — opens the support page. See [Contact support](/en/support/contact-support).
- **Notifications** — the latest incidents and news.
- **Theme and language** — light or dark appearance, and the portal's language.
- **User menu** — **Profile** opens [My account](/en/getting-started/my-account). **Log out** signs you out.

On a phone, the search is an icon that opens the search field.

## The menu bar

- **Organization switcher** at the far left — switch organization. Open it with **Ctrl O** (**⌘O** on a Mac). See [Select organization](/en/getting-started/select-organization).
- **Modules** — the organization's modules. If not all of them fit, the rest are gathered under **Show more modules**.
- **Settings** — for administrators. Here you find the organization's settings and, for partner users, **Partner Admin**.

The organization's settings have their own menu on the left, grouped into **Access**, **Services**, **Appearance** and **Operations & audit**. See [Organization & Teams](/en/organization/overview).

## Module cards

Each module is shown as a card. Select **Open module** to open it. With the star you choose **Add to favorites**. Favorites appear first in the navigation and on the dashboard.

## What you see

Which modules and actions appear is controlled by two things:

1. **What the organization has enabled** — a module that is **Not activated** is shown greyed out and cannot be opened. A module that is **Hidden** is not shown at all. Read more under [Modules](/en/organization/modules).
2. **Your role** — your permissions determine what you can see and do.

::note
Missing a feature you expect? It may be turned off for the organization or outside your role. Contact your administrator.
::

## Incidents and news

Under **Active incidents** you see ongoing disruptions. **View all incidents** opens the **Operations** page. See [Service status](/en/support/service-status).
