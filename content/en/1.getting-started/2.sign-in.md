# Sign in

Signing in always starts with your email address. The portal then decides whether you sign in with a password or via your organization's SSO.

## Sign in with email and password

1. Go to the portal's sign-in page.
2. Enter your email address and select **Continue**.
3. Enter your password.
4. Confirm the security check if it appears.
5. Select **Log in**.

If you entered the wrong address, select **Change email address** to start over.

### Two-step verification (MFA)

If you have turned on two-step verification, the portal asks you after the password to "Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app." Type the code under **Verification code**. It is submitted automatically once you have entered six digits; otherwise select **Verify**.

If you can't reach the app, select **Use a backup code**. Enter one of your backup codes under **Backup code** ("Enter one of your backup codes. Each code works once.") and select **Verify**. A backup code is never submitted automatically. **Use the authenticator app** takes you back to the code from the app.

**Having trouble logging in?** shows help. If you have lost both your phone and your backup codes: "Contact your provider or administrator, who can reset your two-step verification."

### If the portal requires two-step verification

If your organization, provider or distributor requires two-step verification and you don't have it, you set it up right after the password. The **Turn on two-step verification** page says why, for example "{name} requires two-step verification. Turn it on now to continue – it takes a minute."

1. **1. Get an authenticator app** — for example Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator on your phone.
2. **2. Scan the QR code with the app.** If you can't scan it, you can type the code shown below the QR code into the app.
3. **3. Enter the code the app shows** and select **Verify and continue**.
4. Your backup codes are shown once: "Save these backup codes in a safe place!" Select **Copy codes** or **Download**.
5. Tick "I have saved the backup codes somewhere safe." and select **Continue**.

The requirement applies to signing in with a password. A sign-in via SSO counts as meeting it.

::note
If you are signed out with the message "Your account now requires two-step verification. Sign in again and you will set it up right away." the requirement was added while you were signed in. Sign in again and follow the steps above.
::

### Choose a new password

If an administrator has reset your password, **Choose a new password** is shown after you sign in: "An administrator reset your password. Choose a new one to continue."

1. Enter the password under **New password** and **Confirm new password**.
2. Select **Save and sign in**.

The password must meet the requirements shown: at least 12 characters, with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one digit and one special character. It must be different from your current one.

### Error messages when signing in

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Wrong email or password. Try again." | Check the address and the password. Use **Forgot password?** if you don't remember it. |
| "Too many attempts. Wait a moment and try again." | Wait a few minutes before trying again. |
| "The account is disabled. Contact your administrator." | The account is turned off. Ask an administrator to enable it. |
| "The sign-in took too long. Sign in again." | You start over from the password. The email address is kept. |
| "Too many incorrect codes. Sign in again." | Start over and enter a new code from the app, or a backup code. |

## Sign in via SSO (single sign-on)

If your organization has enabled [single sign-on](/en/organization/sso), you sign in with your usual organization account.

1. Enter your email address and select **Continue**.
2. If your organization has one sign-in, you are sent straight to your identity provider. If there are several, a button is shown for each, for example **Log in with Microsoft Entra ID**.
3. Sign in with your identity provider. You are then returned to the portal.

If the organization requires SSO, you can't sign in with a password. You then see the message "The organization requires SSO. Use SSO login."

A sign-in through an organization's or provider's own SSO only covers that place. If your account reaches more organizations, you may need to sign in again to open them, see [Select organization](/en/getting-started/select-organization).

### Link the sign-in to your account

If you sign in via SSO and that sign-in is not yet linked to an account in the portal, **Link the sign-in to your account** is shown. You then show that the account is yours by signing in with the account's password. "The sign-in service's email address is not proof on its own."

1. Enter **Email address of your account** and **Password**.
2. Select **Sign in to link**. If the account has two-step verification, also enter **Code from your authenticator app**.
3. Check the account and select **Link to {email address}**.

After that you sign in via SSO as usual.

## Sign in with Microsoft

Below the email field, after "or", there is a **Sign in with Microsoft** button (Work or school account). With it you don't need to type your email address; you pick your Microsoft account directly.

The button works for organizations that have connected Microsoft Entra ID to CloudPortal. If your organization isn't connected, you see "Your organization is not connected to Microsoft sign-in here. Enter your email address instead." Sign in with your email address instead.

::tip
The email field always works, even when your organization uses Microsoft. Use it if the button doesn't take you to the right place, for example if you are a guest in another organization's Microsoft environment.
::

## You need a portal account first

Signing in via SSO or Microsoft normally gives you no access on its own. You get in if you already have an account and are an active member of the organization, for example after an invitation. Otherwise you see the message:

> You have been authenticated, but no access context could be determined. Contact your administrator.

In that case, ask an administrator in your organization to invite you.

::note
Which sign-in options are shown depends on how your organization is configured. If the SSO option is missing but you expect it, contact your administrator.
::

## Sign in on your organization's own domain

If your organization or provider has its own domain for the portal, the sign-in page there shows their logo and colors. You sign in the same way as at the portal's main address.

You stay on the own domain if your account only reaches what the domain's owner is responsible for, for example when you are only a member of that organization. If the account reaches more, you sign in at the portal's main address instead:

- If you sign in with a password, you see "Your account has access that requires signing in at the portal's main address." Select **Sign in at the main address**.
- If you sign in via SSO, you land straight on the main address. It says "Your account has access that requires signing in at the portal's main address, so you were signed in here instead of at {address}."

If sign-in on the domain is temporarily off, you see "Signing in at this address is not available right now. Sign in at the portal's main address instead." If an SSO sign-in could not be completed, you see "The sign-in could not be completed at this address. Start the sign-in again."

You can also be signed out of the own domain:

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Your account has been given access that requires signing in at the portal's main address, so you have been signed out here." | You have been given access to more since you signed in. Sign in at the portal's main address. |
| "This address has changed since you signed in, so you have been signed out. Sign in again." | The domain's settings have changed. Sign in again. |
| "Sign in again to continue at this address." | Sign in again. |

::note
Signing out of the portal does not sign you out of Microsoft. A new Microsoft sign-in may therefore go through without asking for the password. Sign out of Microsoft too on a shared computer.
::

How the domain is set up is described in [Custom domain](/en/organization/custom-domain).

## Accept an invitation

An invitation comes by email. The link opens the **Accept organization invitation** page. It shows the organization, your role, when the invitation expires and who invited you. You become a member only once you have accepted yourself, even if you already have an account.

- **You already have an account.** The page says "Log in to accept the invitation." Select **Go to login** and sign in with the invited account. You then return to the invitation, which is accepted. If the **Accept invitation** button is shown, select it. If the account signs in via SSO, sign in there.
- **You have no account.** Fill in **Full name**, **Password** and **Confirm password** and select **Create account and join**. The password must meet the requirements shown.
- **You have no account, and the address belongs to the organization's Microsoft sign-in.** You don't create a password: "You accept the invitation by signing in with your Microsoft account ({email address}). No password is needed here." Select **Continue with Microsoft**.
- **You are signed in with the wrong account.** The page says "You are logged in as {email address} but the invitation was sent to {invited address}. Log out and log in with the correct account to continue."

An invitation as an external guest shows "You are invited as an external guest", which permissions you get and until which date. See [External guests](/en/organization/external-guests).

## Forgot password

Select **Forgot password?** in the password step and enter your email address. You'll receive an email with a link to reset your password.

## No access

If your account has nothing to open, you land on the **No access** page:

> Your account has no organization, tenant or platform area to open right now. If you were just given access, it can take a moment to show up. Otherwise it has been removed, for example when your account was taken out of a group in your directory.

Select **Try again** if you were just given access. Otherwise select **Contact support** or ask your administrator for access. **Sign out** signs you out.

## Next steps

Once signed in, choose which [organization](/en/getting-started/select-organization) you want to work in. Your profile and your sign-in are under [My account](/en/getting-started/my-account).
