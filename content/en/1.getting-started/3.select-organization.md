# Select organization

Your account can have access to several organizations. You always work in **one** organization at a time, and the selected organization determines which domains, certificates, members and settings you see.

## Selecting an organization at sign-in

If you have access to more than one organization, you choose which one to work in after signing in. If you only have one organization, it's selected automatically.

## Switching organization

You can switch the active organization at any time using the organization switcher in the portal's top bar. Search with the field at the top of the list, or select **View all organizations** to see the full list. When you switch, all content updates to the new organization.

::warning
Double-check which organization you're working in before making changes, especially if you manage several customers. Changes always apply to the active organization.
::

### Organizations that require SSO

If an organization requires SSO and you signed in with a password, it is dimmed and marked **SSO** in the list. Sign in via the organization's SSO to be able to select it.

## For partner users: how the list is grouped

If you work at a provider or distributor, the organization switcher is grouped:

- **Providers and distributors** you belong to are shown at the top, with your role. Under each one are the organizations you reach through it.
- **Organizations** holds your own memberships that don't belong to any of the groups above.

The same organization can appear under more than one provider or distributor if you reach it in several ways. You then choose which route to use.

### Icons

| Icon (text when you hover over it) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Access via MSP role** | You are not a member of the organization, but reach it through an MSP role at your provider or distributor. |
| **Delegation** | The organization has lent you permissions for a limited time. The end date is shown in parentheses. Read more under [Delegations](/en/organization/delegations). |

### "as {tenant}"

When you work in an organization you are not a member of, but reach via an MSP role or a delegation, the organization switcher in the top bar shows **as** followed by the name of the provider or distributor, for example "Customer Ltd as Provider Ltd". It shows who you are acting as. The addition is not shown on small screens.

MSP access only applies while you act as the provider or distributor that granted it. To work in the same organization through another provider or distributor, select the organization under that one in the list.

## No organization?

If you don't have access to any organization, you may need to complete an organization setup or wait for an administrator to grant you access. If you've received an invitation, follow the link in the invitation email to accept it.
