# Select organization

Your account can have access to several organizations. You always work in **one** organization at a time, and the selected organization determines which domains, certificates, members and settings you see.

## Selecting an organization at sign-in

If you have access to more than one organization, the **Select organization** page is shown after you sign in. If you only have one organization, it's selected automatically.

You can search the list and use the keyboard: ↑ ↓ to select, ↵ to open and esc to go back. A long list is shown a part at a time. Select **Show more ({count} of {total})** to see more.

## Switching organization

The organization switcher is at the far left of the menu bar. Open it with a click or with **Ctrl O** (**⌘O** on a Mac). Type to search in the "Search organizations and consoles…" field, and open a row with ↵ or a click. When you switch, all content updates to the new organization.

::warning
Double-check which organization you're working in before making changes, especially if you manage several customers. Changes always apply to the active organization.
::

### How the list is shown

- **Short lists** are shown in full.
- **From 8 rows**, **Recent** is shown at the top, with what you opened lately. The rest of the list follows below.
- **From 26 rows**, only **Recent** and **Consoles** — your providers and distributors — are shown. You find other organizations by searching. **View all ({count})** at the bottom opens the Select organization page with the whole list.

Each organization is shown with its symbol, or with initials if it has none. Your role is shown under the name. See [Branding](/en/organization/branding).

### Organizations that require SSO

If an organization requires SSO and you signed in another way, its row is locked and marked "Requires SSO sign-in". If you choose it, you see "{name} requires sign-in through the organization’s SSO." Select **Sign in with SSO** to sign in via the organization's SSO.

The same applies to a provider or distributor that requires SSO. Its console is locked, and the customers you reach through it are only shown once you have signed in via its SSO.

An organization marked **Suspended** cannot be opened.

## For partner users: how the list is grouped

If you work at a provider or distributor, the organization switcher is grouped:

- **Providers and distributors** you belong to are shown as consoles, for example "Provider console · Admin" or "Distributor console · User". Under each one are the organizations you reach through it.
- **Organizations** holds your own memberships that don't belong to any of the groups above.

The same organization can appear under more than one provider or distributor if you reach it in several ways. You then choose which route to use.

### Icons and badges

| Icon or badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Access via MSP role** (text when you hover over the icon) | You are not a member of the organization, but reach it through an MSP role at your provider or distributor. |
| **Delegation** (text when you hover over the icon) | The organization has lent you permissions for a limited time. The end date is shown as "until {date}". Read more under [Delegations](/en/organization/delegations). |
| **Guest** | You are an external guest in the organization. The end date is shown as "until {date}". Read more under [External guests](/en/organization/external-guests). |

### "as {tenant}"

When you work in an organization you are not a member of, but reach via an MSP role or a delegation, the organization switcher shows **as** followed by the name of the provider or distributor, for example "Customer Ltd as Provider Ltd". It shows who you are acting as. The addition is not shown on small screens.

MSP access only applies while you act as the provider or distributor that granted it. To work in the same organization through another provider or distributor, select the organization under that one in the list.

## Missing an organization?

If you signed in through an organization's or provider's own SSO, that sign-in only covers that place. The list then shows **Missing an organization?**. Open it to see why, for example:

- "You signed in through {name}. That sign-in covers only {name}."
- "You signed in through {name}. That sign-in covers {name} and the organizations you reach from there."

Select **Sign in again** and sign in with a sign-in that covers where you want to work, for example your password or that organization's own sign-in.

If you have no access to any organization at all, see **No access** under [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in). If you've received an invitation, follow the link in the invitation email to accept it.

All the organizations you reach, and what gives you the access, are shown under [My account](/en/getting-started/my-account) → **Access**.
