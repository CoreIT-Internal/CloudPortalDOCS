# My account

Under **My account** you see and change what applies to you yourself. Nothing here affects other users.

Open the user menu at the far right of the top bar and select **Profile**. The menu on the left has the pages **Profile**, **Sign-in and security**, **Access**, **Preferences**, **My requests** and **Hidden notices**.

## Profile

"How you appear in the portal."

- **Name** — change the name and select **Save name**. If you sign in with Microsoft, the name can't be changed here: "Your name comes from Microsoft Entra at every sign-in. Change it in Entra."
- **Email** — "Your email address is your username and cannot be changed here."
- **Profile picture** — choose **Picture from Microsoft** (or **Picture from your sign-in** for another SSO) or **Initials**. The picture is updated at every sign-in. If you don't sign in with Microsoft or SSO, the portal shows your initials.

## Sign-in and security

"How you sign in, and where you are signed in right now."

### Ways to sign in

The list shows what you can sign in with, for example **Microsoft**, another SSO and **Password**. The one you are using right now is marked **In use now**.

### Change password

If the account has a password, you change it here:

1. Enter **Current password**.
2. Enter **New password** and **Confirm new password**. The password must meet the requirements shown.
3. Select **Update password**.

If you are signed in with Microsoft, you need to sign in with the password to change it. If the account has no password, you only sign in with SSO, for example Microsoft.

### Two-step verification

"A code from an app on your phone when you sign in with a password. Someone with your password cannot get in without the phone."

**Turn it on:**

1. Select **Turn on two-step verification**.
2. Follow the steps **1. Get an authenticator app**, **2. Scan the QR code with the app** and **3. Enter the code the app shows**.
3. Select **Verify and turn on**.
4. Save the backup codes, which are shown once, with **Copy codes** or **Download**. Tick "I have saved the backup codes somewhere safe." and select **Done**.

**When it is on** you see the method and how many backup codes you have left ("{count} left").

- **Make new backup codes** — enter a code from the app or a backup code. The old backup codes stop working.
- **Turn off** — enter a code from the app or a backup code. Your other sign-ins are signed out.

If it has been a while since you signed in, the portal first asks **Confirm with your password**. Enter **Password**, and the code if the account has two-step verification, and select **Confirm**.

Two-step verification here applies to signing in with a password, and the section is only shown if the account has a password. If you are signed in with Microsoft, you sign in with the password to change it.

::tip
If you have lost both your phone and your backup codes, your provider or administrator can reset your two-step verification.
::

### Active sign-ins

"Browsers and devices where you are signed in. Sign out any you do not recognise."

- **This device** is the one you are using now.
- **Sign out** signs out a single sign-in.
- **Sign out all others** signs out all except this one. Confirm with **Sign out**.

## Access

"The organizations you can open, and what gives you access."

- **Direct member** — organizations you are a member of yourself, with your role. If you are an external guest, it says **Guest**.
- **Providers and distributors** you belong to — your MSP roles and where they apply, for example "all customers of {name}" or "selected organizations ({count})", and the organizations you reach through them.
- **Delegated access** — "Time-limited access an organization has lent you." The end date is shown as "until {date}".

**Switch organization** opens the [Select organization](/en/getting-started/select-organization) page.

## Preferences

"Language, appearance and favorites. Only for you."

- **Language** — the portal's language.
- **Appearance** — **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. System follows your device settings.
- **Favorites** — modules you mark as favorites appear first in the navigation and on the dashboard. **Remove** removes one favorite, and **Clear all favorites** removes them all.

## My requests

"Requests you have sent from the support form. Replies come by email to {email address}."

Each request is shown with its subject, reference number, category and time. On the right it says where it was sent, for example "Sent to {email address}". If it says **Not delivered**, the request never reached support. Send it again, or contact support another way.

"The portal only knows that a request was sent. Replies and follow-up happen by email."

**New request** opens the support form. See [Contact support](/en/support/contact-support).

## Hidden notices

Here you find incidents you have hidden with **Hide for me**. Select **Show again** to show one again. See [Service status](/en/support/service-status).
