# Service status

Here you can see whether anything is going on right now – service disruptions, maintenance, and other important information. Service status is shown directly in the portal.

## Where to find service status

1. Open the **Operations** page. Select **View all incidents** on the dashboard, or **View all** in the notifications in the top bar.
2. The page shows **Active incidents** and **Latest news** from your provider, your distributor and your own organization.
3. Click a message to see details.

## Understanding an incident

Each incident has a severity and a status.

### Severity

- **Critical** – serious impact.
- **Outage** – the service is not working as it should.
- **Maintenance** – ongoing or planned maintenance.
- **Notice** – an advisory with no direct service impact.
- **Planned** – a planned action, often with a start and end time.

### Status

- **Active** – the message is ongoing.
- **Resolved** – the problem has been fixed.
- **Closed** – the matter is concluded.

In the detail view you also see times: when the message **starts**, **ends**, and was **resolved**. **From** shows who the message comes from.

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance is published in advance as an incident, often with severity **Maintenance** or **Planned** and a specified start and end time. Keep an eye on the Operations page ahead of announced times.

## Notifications

You are alerted to incidents and news via notifications in the portal.

- Open **Notifications** in the top bar to see the latest **incidents** and **news**.
- Select **Mark all as read** to mark them as read.

## Hide an incident

You can hide an incident that doesn't concern you with **Hide for me**. It only applies to you.

Hidden incidents are in the **Hidden incidents** section on the Operations page, and under **My account** → **Hidden notices**. Select **Show again** to show one again. See [My account](/en/getting-started/my-account).

## Your organization's own incidents

Incidents and news can also come from your own organization. Administrators manage them under **Settings** → **Operations**. The **Incidents** page has three tabs:

- **Incoming** — messages from the provider and the distributor. Select **Hide for me**, or **Hide for organization** to hide a message for everyone. The portal asks first: "No one in {organization} sees the status message in the portal until someone shows it again under Hidden status messages." **Show for organization** shows it again.
- **Organization's own** — internal incidents shown only to the organization. Select **Create incident**. "The status message is published at once and shown to everyone in {organization}." A message can be edited, archived or deleted.
- **News** — "News your organisation’s members see in their feed." Select **Write news**. A news post is saved as a draft and published in a separate step.

## When the information isn't enough

If you experience a problem that isn't listed among the active incidents:

1. Read [Errors and troubleshooting](/en/support/errors-and-troubleshooting) – it may be something you can resolve yourself.
2. If the problem persists, go to [Contact support](/en/support/contact-support).

::warning
Service status reflects what providers, distributors and your own organization have published in the portal. An individual service can be affected before a message has been posted – please report it to support if you see a disruption that hasn't been announced.
::
