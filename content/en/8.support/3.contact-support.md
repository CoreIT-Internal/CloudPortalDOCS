# Contact support

If you cannot find the answer in the documentation or under [Errors and troubleshooting](/en/support/errors-and-troubleshooting), support will help you. The support page in the portal always shows the contact details that apply to you.

The support page requires signing in. If you cannot get into the portal, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page or contact your administrator.

## Send a request from the portal

1. Open **Support** in the portal. The link is in the top menu.
2. Choose what the request is about: **Question**, **Something is not working**, **Access and permissions** or **Other**.
3. Write a subject and describe what you need help with.
4. Select **Send request**.

You get a reference number, for example `CP-7KQ4MX`, and the answer comes to your email address. Include the reference if you get in touch about the same request again.

::note
**This is sent along automatically,** so support does not have to ask: your name and email address, the organization you work in, the page you came from, the time and which browser you use. You see the list under **Sent along with it** before you send.
::

::tip
Go to the support page straight from the page where the problem occurred. That page is then included in the request.
::

### If the request cannot be sent

The portal says so right away, and what you wrote is kept. You can send the same request from your own email program with the link shown instead.

You can send five requests an hour. If you need to send more, wait a while.

### If the form is not shown

- **Your support does not take requests through the portal.** Use the phone number or contact details on the page.
- **No support is set.** Contact whoever gave you access to the portal.

## Turn to your administrator first in these cases

Some requests are solved fastest by your own organization's administrator:

- **You need more permissions**, for example to a module or feature.
- **You need to be added or removed** as a user.
- **Two-factor authentication needs to be reset** because you have lost both the app and the backup codes.
- **SSO sign-in** for your organization. Contact whoever administers your identity provider.

## Write a request that can be solved right away

Support already sees who you are and where you were. So describe:

1. **What you were trying to do** and what happened instead.
2. **What you expected** to happen.
3. **The exact error message**, word for word if you can.

## Before you get in touch

Many problems can be solved right away:

- Read [Errors and troubleshooting](/en/support/errors-and-troubleshooting).
- Check [Service status](/en/support/service-status). The fault may be due to a known disruption or planned maintenance.
- Reload the page and try again.
